# Animated videos

This folder holds two animated videos in portrait shape for phone screens and each video has voices and music and subtitles. An AI chatbot (Claude) drew and voiced both videos with computer code and nobody else has rechecked the videos.

## The two videos

- [stomper-food-video.mp4](stomper-food-video.mp4) runs 4 minutes 29 seconds and tells a story about a person with twenty dollars left who buys a pile of clearance food and stomps the food on camera because people who click in anger pay for the views. On Thanksgiving the person brings a bag of food to a cashier who skipped lunch and stomps the bag on her doorstep for more views and then hands her cash from the view money. The scene is played flat and calm for laughs and the dollar amounts in the story are invented.
- [love-enemies-video.mp4](love-enemies-video.mp4) runs 2 minutes 47 seconds and shows a teacher answering the question of how a person can unify with people who want that person dead. The answer is to feed and shelter the enemy and to withdraw to a safe distance from a raised fist because a person with a full life has much to lose by shedding blood.

## The spoken words as plain text

A person or an AI agent who cannot play video can read every spoken line in these two text files.

- [stomper-words.txt](stomper-words.txt) holds every line spoken in the stomper video with the name of the speaker in front of each line.
- [love-enemies-words.txt](love-enemies-words.txt) holds every line spoken in the love your enemies video with the name of the speaker in front of each line.
