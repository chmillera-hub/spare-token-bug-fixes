The rule. Every room starts with four closed doors. Each room reads one number and opens one door: 1 opens the north door and 2 opens the east door and 3 opens the south door and 4 opens the west door. The cycle then repeats so 5 is north and 6 is east and 7 is south and 8 is west and 9 is north and 0 is west. A door opened from either side joins the two rooms. A door that points outside the grid joins nothing. The numbers fill the rooms row by row starting at the top left room.

The scores. A region is a group of rooms joined by open doors. A top to bottom crossing is a region that touches both the top row and the bottom row. A side to side crossing is a region that touches both the left column and the right column. The longest walk is the most rooms a walker can visit without entering a room twice. The maze score is the longest walk plus the number of rooms along one side for each crossing.
