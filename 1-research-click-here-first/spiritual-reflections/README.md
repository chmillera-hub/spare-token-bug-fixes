# Spiritual reflections

This folder holds scripture passages that the author of this repository collected together with the author's reflections on the passages.

- [spiritual-quotes.md](spiritual-quotes.md) holds 45 entries of scripture passages in the wording of the World English Bible with the author's reflections which read many passages as pictures of listening to emotions and speaking about suffering and caring for people in need.
- [garden-prayer-story.md](garden-prayer-story.md) holds a story in which Jesus heals the ear of a man in the arresting party and then speaks the whole prayer of John chapter 17 to the disciples while the soldiers stand watching with torches.
- [times-square-return-story.md](times-square-return-story.md) holds a story in which Jesus returns to Times Square and refuses to use violence and is stalled by a parking meter and a once a month food pantry and the risk that armed officers would hurt bystanders.
- [small-group-text-exchange.md](small-group-text-exchange.md) holds an anonymized text message exchange in which the author asks two service workers from a church for groups of two to four people and is told that the Bible study gathers 15 to 30 people and that the meetings should stop and the page ends with the author's note on guilt and a reflection on a withheld review page link.
