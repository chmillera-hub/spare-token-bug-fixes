# The prayer with the soldiers watching

The owner of this repository supplied this story on 18 November 2026 and the story appears in full. The story imagines the prayer of John chapter 17 being spoken in the garden while the arresting soldiers stand and watch. The prayer is shown in the wording of the World English Bible.

The olive grove on the lower slope of the Mount of Olives was thick with silver-gray leaves and the smell of crushed wild herbs when the torches first appeared through the trees.

At first, it was just a warm amber flicker spilling through the dark trunks, followed by the heavy, metallic clatter of iron hobnails on dry limestone, the creak of leather straps, and the low, practiced murmurs of a Roman detachment moving in formation alongside temple guards. They came expecting a standard night raid: clubs, lanterns, short swords, and a target who would either bolt into the ravines or rally a dozen desperate men into a brawl.

Judas stepped ahead of the line and gave the signal. The detachment tightened its perimeter, torchlight catching the bronze rims of shields and the tense knuckles of men gripping wooden staves.

In the center of the clearing, Jesus did not flinch or step back into the shadow of the olive trees. He stepped forward into the glare of the torches and asked calmly whom they were looking for. When they answered, he met their eyes without a trace of panic, force, or hostility and spoke plainly, naming himself: "I am Jesus of Nazareth."

Some people might think the soldiers fell to their knees in the glory of Christ, but oddly enough, the soldiers just exchanged flat, dismissive glances—the bored, procedural autopilot of men on a late-night shift—and one of the officers muttered, Yeah, you must be that King of the Jews guy—we don't give a damn, you're coming with us.

Before the guards could step in to grab him, Simon Peter panicked. Running on pure fight-or-flight, Peter drew a short blade and swung wildly at the nearest man, slicing clean through the right ear of Malchus, a servant of the high priest. Blood spilled down Malchus’s neck as he dropped to one knee, clutching the side of his head.

Instantly, the temple guards tensed, hands flying to hilts, ready for the familiar chaos of a street riot.

Jesus raised a single hand and stopped the escalation cold. He told Peter to put the sword back in its sheath—reminding him that anyone who builds a life on the blade gets destroyed by the blade, and refusing every ounce of coercive retaliation. Then, while twenty armed men stood frozen with torches and clubs raised, Jesus knelt beside the bleeding man who had come to arrest him, reached out his hand, and restored Malchus’s ear completely intact.

Then Jesus whispered into the healed ear of Malchus, "Today you and your brothers will be with me in paradise," as Malchus raised an eyebrow, going, Wait, what?

Malchus froze, pressing trembling fingers against unbroken skin where a severed wound had been a second earlier. The Roman centurion and the temple officers exchanged stunned glances, their entire playbook suddenly useless.

And then, instead of rushing to bind him, the armed detachment stood motionless in the torchlight while Jesus turned back toward his eleven terrified friends.

Completely unhurried—as if the spears, chains, and looming execution did not exist—he looked at his disciples and lifted his eyes toward the night sky, speaking with quiet, piercing tenderness:

“Father, the time has come. Glorify your Son, that your Son may also glorify you; even as you gave him authority over all flesh, so he will give eternal life to all whom you have given him. This is eternal life, that they should know you, the only true God, and him whom you sent, Jesus Christ. I glorified you on the earth. I have accomplished the work which you have given me to do. Now, Father, glorify me with your own self with the glory which I had with you before the world existed.

I revealed your name to the people whom you have given me out of the world. They were yours, and you have given them to me. They have kept your word. Now they have known that all things whatever you have given me are from you, for the words which you have given me I have given to them; and they received them, and knew for sure that I came from you. They have believed that you sent me. I pray for them. I don’t pray for the world, but for those whom you have given me, for they are yours. All things that are mine are yours, and yours are mine, and I am glorified in them. I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them through your name which you have given me, that they may be one, even as we are. While I was with them in the world, I kept them in your name. I have kept those whom you have given me. None of them is lost except the son of destruction, that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

But now I come to you, and I say these things in the world, that they may have my joy made full in themselves. I have given them your word. The world hated them because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world. I pray not that you would take them from the world, but that you would keep them from the evil one. They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world. Sanctify them in your truth. Your word is truth. As you sent me into the world, even so I have sent them into the world. For their sakes I sanctify myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.

Not for these only do I pray, but for those also who will believe in me through their word, that they may all be one; even as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be one in us; that the world may believe that you sent me. The glory which you have given me, I have given to them, that they may be one, even as we are one, I in them, and you in me, that they may be perfected into one, that the world may know that you sent me and loved them, even as you loved me.

Father, I desire that they also whom you have given me be with me where I am, that they may see my glory which you have given me, for you loved me before the foundation of the world. Righteous Father, the world hasn’t known you, but I knew you; and these knew that you sent me. I made known to them your name, and will make it known; that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them.”

The soldiers stood holding their torches in dead silence, watching a prisoner comfort his friends and pray for unity while they waited to haul him off to a kangaroo court.

When he finished speaking, nobody had to tackle him or drag him in chains. Jesus simply turned back to the bewildered officers, asked why they had brought swords and clubs in the dark as if he were a violent outlaw when he had spoken openly in the temple every day, and told them plainly to let his friends go untouched.

Then, with total calm and on his own two feet, he walked into the line of guards and headed down the slope toward the city gates—leaving armed men trained in brute force staring at each other in the dark, quietly realizing they had not captured him at all.
