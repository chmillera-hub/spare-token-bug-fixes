# Mosiah chapter 3 in modern English

> **About this page:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this page on 17 November 2026 by rewording chapter 3 of the Book of Mosiah from the Book of Mormon into modern English one verse at a time. Every detail of each verse was kept and no other person has rechecked the rewording. The page named [mosiah-3.md](mosiah-3.md) holds the same chapter in the original wording. A king is speaking to his people and from verse 3 onward the king repeats what an angel told him.

1 And again my brothers I ask for your attention because I have more to say to you. I have things to tell you about what lies ahead.

2 The things I will tell you were made known to me by an angel from God. The angel said to me: Awake. I awoke and saw the angel standing in front of me.

3 The angel said to me: Awake and hear the words I will tell you because I am here to declare to you glad news of great joy.

4 The Lord has heard your prayers and has judged your righteousness and has sent me to declare to you that you may rejoice. You may also declare the news to your people so that your people too may be filled with joy.

5 The time is near and not far away when the Lord Omnipotent will descend from heaven with power to live among humankind. The Lord Omnipotent reigns and was and is from all eternity to all eternity. He will live in a body of clay and will go out among people working mighty miracles such as healing the sick and raising the dead and causing the lame to walk and the blind to receive their sight and the deaf to hear and curing every kind of disease.

6 He will cast out devils which are the evil spirits that live in the hearts of humankind.

7 Look and see that he will suffer temptations and bodily pain and hunger and thirst and exhaustion. He will suffer more of each one than a human being can endure without dying. Blood will seep out of every pore of his skin because his anguish over the wickedness and the vile deeds of his people will be so great.

8 He will be called Jesus Christ and the Son of God and the Father of heaven and earth and the Creator of all things from the beginning. His mother will be called Mary.

9 He arrives among his own people so that salvation might reach humankind through faith in his name. Even after all of this his own people will consider him only a man and will say that he has a devil and will whip him and will crucify him.

10 He will rise from the dead on the third day and he stands to judge the world. All these things are done so that a righteous judgment might be given to humankind.

11 His blood also atones for the sins of those who have fallen through the transgression of Adam and who died without knowing the will of God for them or who sinned in ignorance.

12 But sorrow and more sorrow to the person who knows that he is rebelling against God! Salvation reaches no such person except through repentance and faith in the Lord Jesus Christ.

13 The Lord God has sent his holy prophets among all of humankind to declare these things to every family and nation and language. Whoever believes that Christ will arrive can then receive forgiveness of sins and rejoice with very great joy as though Christ had already arrived among them.

14 Yet the Lord God saw that his people were a stubborn people and he gave them a law which is the law of Moses.

15 He showed his people many signs and wonders and symbols and foreshadowings about his arrival and holy prophets also spoke to his people about his arrival. Yet his people closed their hearts and did not understand that the law of Moses accomplishes nothing except through the atonement of his blood.

16 Even if little ones were able to sin the little ones could not be saved. But I tell you that little ones are blessed because just as little ones fall through Adam or through nature so the blood of Christ atones for their sins.

17 Moreover I tell you that no other name will be given and no other way or means by which salvation can reach humankind. Salvation is found only in and through the name of Christ the Lord Omnipotent.

18 He judges and his judgment is just. The baby who dies in infancy does not perish. But people drink damnation to their own souls unless they humble themselves and become like little ones and believe that salvation was and is and will be in and through the atoning blood of Christ the Lord Omnipotent.

19 The natural man is an enemy to God and has been since the fall of Adam and will be forever and ever unless he yields to the invitations of the Holy Spirit and puts off the natural man and becomes a saint through the atonement of Christ the Lord. Such a person becomes like a little one who is submissive and meek and humble and patient and full of love and willing to submit to all things that the Lord sees fit to lay upon that person just as a little one submits to a father.

20 Moreover I tell you that the time will arrive when the knowledge of a Savior will spread through every nation and family and language and people.

21 When that time arrives nobody will be found blameless before God except little ones. Everyone else is found blameless only through repentance and faith in the name of the Lord God Omnipotent.

22 Even at this time when you have taught your people the things that the Lord your God has commanded you your people are no longer found blameless in the sight of God except according to the words I have spoken to you.

23 And now I have spoken the words that the Lord God commanded me.

24 And the Lord says this: These words will stand as a bright testimony against this people on the judgment day. Every person will be judged by these words according to that person's works whether the works are good or whether the works are evil.

25 If the works are evil then the people are left to an awful view of their own guilt and vile deeds. That view causes the people to shrink from the presence of the Lord into a state of misery and endless torment from which they can never return. So they have drunk damnation to their own souls.

26 They have drunk from the cup of the wrath of God. Justice could no more withhold that cup from them than justice could deny that Adam would fall because Adam ate the forbidden fruit. So mercy could have no claim on them ever again.

27 Their torment is like a lake of fire and brimstone whose flames cannot be put out and whose smoke rises forever and ever. This is what the Lord has commanded me. Amen.
