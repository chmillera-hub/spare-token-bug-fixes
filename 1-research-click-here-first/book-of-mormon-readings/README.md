# Book of Mormon readings

This folder holds chapters of the Book of Mormon in the original wording and in modern English.

- [mosiah-3.md](mosiah-3.md) holds chapter 3 of the Book of Mosiah in the original wording where a king tells his people what an angel said about a savior who will heal the sick and suffer hunger and thirst and exhaustion.
- [modern-mosiah-3.md](modern-mosiah-3.md) holds the same chapter reworded into modern English one verse at a time with every detail kept.
