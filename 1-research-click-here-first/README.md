# Research

Every item in this folder is written for a person to read and each line below says in one sentence what the item contains.

- [voynich-manuscript-findings.md](voynich-manuscript-findings.md) tells who probably made the unreadable Voynich manuscript in the early 1400s (five scribes and fifteen calfskins and a castle drawing that points to northern Italy) and how the book was built and what spelling-pattern tests add.
- [voynich-geoguess-clue-sweep.md](voynich-geoguess-clue-sweep.md) guesses where the Voynich manuscript was made by reading eight drawing clues such as a crossbow archer and forked castle walls and baggy sleeves the way a map-guessing player reads a street photo.
- [word-evolution-experiments.md](word-evolution-experiments.md) shows what happens when text is copied with small mistakes over and over while a reader picks which copies survive.
- [hiding-a-book-by-splitting-pages.md](hiding-a-book-by-splitting-pages.md) shows how much sense is left when a 1000-page biology book is dealt into four piles in four different ways.
- [mobius-strip-picture-of-physics](mobius-strip-picture-of-physics) is a document that pictures strange behaviors in physics with twisted paper ribbons and uses no equations.
- [writing-rules-for-clear-chatbot-replies.md](writing-rules-for-clear-chatbot-replies.md) is a set of writing rules with before and after examples and the file ending in plain-text.txt holds the same rules for easy copying.
- [try-the-word-evolution-program.md](try-the-word-evolution-program.md) explains two ways to run the live word evolution program with sliders and ten separate agents and also covers a second version where the agents build strings out of short quotes from the World English Bible.
- [bible-selections-word-evolution.md](bible-selections-word-evolution.md) compares what copying agents build from three Bible selections (41 well-known passages and ten whole books and only the words of Jesus) and gives two ways to run the live program.
- [record-of-the-chat-behind-the-research.md](record-of-the-chat-behind-the-research.md) lists every request Charles made in the chat that produced the research pages in his own words with a short note on what the chatbot did and gives the public link to the full word-for-word chat.
- [bible-key-phrase-evolution.md](bible-key-phrase-evolution.md) reports what happens when the Bible is chopped into short key phrases and copying agents put stretches of the text back together and gives two ways to run the live program.
- [free-bug-fixes-video.md](free-bug-fixes-video.md) points to a short video of 363 free bug fixes for public software projects.

## Live Bible key phrase programs that open in a web browser

Each link opens a live program where ten agents copy and paste short Bible phrases while a reader favors strings whose neighboring phrases are also neighbors in the Bible and each program starts on a different selection of Bible text.

- Bible Key Phrases: Claude Selection starts on 41 well-known passages picked by the AI model: https://claude.ai/artifact/PK8tNEMDZM2eVaCfuNSxdR
- Bible Key Phrases: Charles Version starts on the ten whole books picked by Charles (Psalms and Proverbs and Ecclesiastes and Isaiah and Jeremiah and Matthew and Mark and Luke and John and Romans): https://claude.ai/artifact/4pJdWtCH4ahdFjsb4GVb8Q
- Bible Key Phrases: Jesus Version starts on the words of Jesus in Matthew and Mark and Luke and John: https://claude.ai/artifact/Bzcg2eYnte9cTc7wtpBArz
- A link opens for a visitor only if Charles has turned on sharing for that page in the Claude app and a visitor who sees an access message can use the program file in this folder named bible-key-phrase-evolution-live-program.html.

Warning about a wording change: Charles replaces the name "yahweh" with the word "god" everywhere in the Bible programs and in the newer results as a personal reading choice that helps Charles follow the quotes which means the quotes differ from the World English Bible at every place where the World English Bible prints the name and a reader who wants the original wording can open the World English Bible itself.

Warning about changed wording in the joined Bible passages: the passages that the AI model joined by hand for the Claude selection and the Charles selection and the words of Jesus are not exact Bible quotes because Charles set rules that allow pronouns such as "he" and "him" and "they" to be deleted or swapped for naming words and allow rarely used names such as the names of little-known cities to be replaced with generic words and allow the word "the" and punctuation marks to be added or removed which means a reader who wants the exact wording needs to open the World English Bible itself.
