# Research

Every item in this folder is written for a person to read and the items are grouped into one folder per project and each line below says in one sentence what the item contains.

## Folders (one project each)

- [animated-videos](animated-videos) holds two animated videos with voices and music and subtitles (a story about a person who stomps clearance food for angry clicks and a teaching about feeding an enemy) and plain text files of every spoken line.
- [authority-and-power](authority-and-power) holds reflections on authority and power structures in society which argue from the principle that touching a person without consent is wrong from anyone.
- [bible-quote-builder](bible-quote-builder) holds an experiment that remixes short phrases from the World English Bible into replies to comments and headlines and the folder has its own list that starts with the finished outputs and the rules.
- [book-of-mormon-readings](book-of-mormon-readings) holds chapters of the Book of Mormon in the original wording and in modern English starting with chapter 3 of the Book of Mosiah.
- [christ-voice](christ-voice) holds short scripts where Christ talks with the warrior prince Vegeta (four scripts in word patterns counted from the red-letter words of the World English Bible and three interactions in a two-part shape counted from passages rated as hitting hard).
- [delivery-routes](delivery-routes) holds computer tests of how close 10000 guesses land to the shortest possible delivery route (the traveling salesman problem) which show that blind random routes are 209% longer than the shortest route at 52 stops while routes built by driving to the nearest unvisited stop 9 times out of 10 are 6% longer.
- [emotion-stories](emotion-stories) holds story ideas and reflections by the author of this repository in which emotions such as anger and boredom appear as characters.
- [emotional-framework](emotional-framework) holds a document that the author of this repository wrote which treats each emotion as a member of an inner family and lists 19 emotions with the purpose and need of each one so that a person or an AI agent can look up what an emotion is signaling.
- [kindness-games](kindness-games) holds small computer tournaments about the instruction "love your enemies" which test whether a player who always makes kind moves can do well when the player may leave a harmful partner or try a low-stakes sample game or see a move in advance or check a visible record.
- [lean-math-proofs](lean-math-proofs) holds three computer-checked proofs in the proof language Lean 4 of two small helper steps toward known results about Erdős problems 946 and 1162 (a counting fact about numbers divisible by large squares and a lower bound on the number of subgroups of the symmetric group) which the checking script of the public project Left to Solve accepted.
- [mobius-strip-picture-of-physics](mobius-strip-picture-of-physics) holds a document that pictures strange behaviors in physics with twisted paper ribbons and uses no equations.
- [other-collections](other-collections) holds links to four public GitHub collections made by other people (about 200 math results written by a person and an AI model and a paper about the Navier-Stokes fluid equations and a short animated film and a tool that lets AI coding programs work as research helpers) with one sentence on what each collection says about itself and ideas for a later check.
- [prime-mazes](prime-mazes) holds experiments that turn the digits of prime numbers into mazes which means a maze test where each digit opens one door of a room (80279 primes with 9 digits make one hallway through all 9 rooms when 1 opens north and 91720 when 0 opens north) and a ranking of primes by maze score under each version of the rule and hallway walks and primes that draw neat shapes and a search of random numbers for the highest scores and a list of patterns scored on nine grid sizes and a ranking of sequences by how many grid sizes they max out and an equation search and sequences with equal digits and a master challenge that scores sequences on the circle test and the maze test together and a summary of the patterns and a program that builds a maze one room at a time.
- [prime-richness](prime-richness) holds computer counts of how often prime numbers show up in the outputs of different recipes compared with the plain number line (86 equations ranked by percentage and results about the digit pieces of a prime and 467 primes where every reversed piece is prime) plus a page that checks any typed equation.
- [social-media-vibes](social-media-vibes) holds 81 written reflections on subjects such as loneliness and empty advice and loving an enemy plus counts of what behaviors the commenters in social media threads ask other people to carry out.
- [spiritual-reflections](spiritual-reflections) holds scripture passages collected by the author of this repository in the wording of the World English Bible together with the author's reflections on the passages and two stories about Jesus (a prayer spoken in a garden while soldiers watch and a return to Times Square) and a text message exchange in which the author asks church service workers for small groups and ten reflections in which a written page prays to its reader.
- [squad-rush-game](squad-rush-game) holds a free phone game that runs in a web browser where a squad of blue soldiers shoots up a road through ten stages of gates and cages and a boss and the folder gives the address for playing the game.
- [three-needs](three-needs) holds research about meeting three basic human needs without force (food and shelter and deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection) which means a page of actions a social media user can take and a page of tested nonviolent replacements for six uses of force such as armed police and eviction.
- [unanswered-questions](unanswered-questions) holds experiments where the chatbot hunted for public questions that nobody had answered and then tried to answer the questions (a mistake found in a published number list and a partial answer about an old Turkish coin and sixty-four more).
- [voynich-manuscript](voynich-manuscript) holds three pages about an unreadable book from the early 1400s which cover who probably made the book and where the book was probably made and a second run of the spelling-pattern tests.
- [word-evolution](word-evolution) holds an experiment where text is copied with small mistakes over and over while a reader picks which copies survive and the folder includes a live program with sliders.
- [writing-rules](writing-rules) holds a set of writing rules for clear chatbot replies with before and after examples and a plain text copy of the same rules.
- [zeta-sums](zeta-sums) holds computer search results about the numbers zeta(3) and zeta(5) and zeta(7) and zeta(9) which include two formulas that write zeta(3) purely as sums over powers of the golden ratio and a formula for zeta(5) with no extra pieces in a base near 0.7549 and formulas with no extra pieces for zeta(7) through zeta(15) in other bases and four long formulas with every whole-number coefficient written out.

## Single pages (each page is its own project)

- [chat-record.md](chat-record.md) lists every request the repository author made in the chat that produced the research pages in his own words with a short note on what the chatbot did and gives the public link to the full word-for-word chat.
- [free-bug-fixes-video.md](free-bug-fixes-video.md) points to a short video of 371 free bug fixes for public software projects.
- [hiding-a-book.md](hiding-a-book.md) shows how much sense is left when a 1000-page biology book is dealt into four piles in four different ways.
- [human-needs-as-research-topics.md](human-needs-as-research-topics.md) is a thought experiment that answers the question of what AI systems should work on next by pointing at living people whose needs are not being met such as a person who is lonely or a person in unexplained pain.
- [scattered-pages-reflection.md](scattered-pages-reflection.md) is a short reflection that compares a life told in separate parts to separate listeners with a book dealt into piles and asks who has been handed enough neighboring pages to follow the story.
