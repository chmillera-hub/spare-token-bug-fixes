# Word evolution

This folder holds an experiment where text is copied with small mistakes over and over while a reader picks which copies survive.

- [experiments.md](experiments.md) reports what happened in the experiments.
- [try-the-program.md](try-the-program.md) explains two ways to run the live program with sliders and ten separate agents.
- The file named live-program.html is the program itself and runs in a web browser after downloading.
- [second-pass.md](second-pass.md) reports a second run of two of the tests on 12 November 2026 with fourteen new starting points where the main results came out again and one time range came out wider.
- [bonus-runs.md](bonus-runs.md) reports twenty runs that measure the story bonus and the fresh-words bonus with five starting points each where the story bonus cut scenes and commands in every run and the fresh-words bonus cut repeats in every run.
- [reflections.md](reflections.md) holds reflections by an AI chatbot that connect results of the experiment to people such as why an added letter has to pay for a memory slot and why a reward that arrives after the work is over cannot guide the work.
