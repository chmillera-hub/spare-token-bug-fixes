# Emotional framework

This folder holds a document named The Emotional Framework that the author of this repository wrote. The document treats each emotion as a member of an inner family and gives each emotion a purpose and a need so that a person can work out a plan that cares for the emotion. An AI chatbot (Claude) put the document into page format on 14 November 2026 and wrote this short guide to the folder.

- [emotional-framework.md](emotional-framework.md) is the full document which holds the method for talking with an emotion and a list of 19 emotions with the purpose and need of each one and example monologues and personal reflections.

## How to use the document as a lookup

A person or an AI agent who is writing about an emotion can find the nearest emotion on the list of 19 and read the purpose and the need written under that emotion because the purpose says what the emotion is signaling and the need says what kind of plan would care for the emotion. A word that is not on the list can be matched to the closest entry. For example the word "frustrated" sits close to Annoyance or Anger and the word "worried" sits close to Fear or Doubt and the word "grief" is covered under Sadness.

The 19 emotions on the list are Anger and Annoyance and Boredom and Doubt and Efficiency and Embarrassment and Fear and Frugality and Guilt and Happiness and Humor and Hunger and Impulsivity and Loneliness and Love and Overwhelm and Sadness and Tiredness and Wellness.
