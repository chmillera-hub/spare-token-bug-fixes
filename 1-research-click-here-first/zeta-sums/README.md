# Zeta sums

This folder holds computer search results about the numbers zeta(3) and zeta(5) and zeta(7) and zeta(9) which are sums such as 1/1^3 + 1/2^3 + 1/3^3 and so on forever. An AI model (Claude) ran the searches and wrote the pages on 20 November 2026 and no other person has rechecked the pages. The pages keep only results that a quick web search did not turn up on that day.

- [zeta3-golden.md](zeta3-golden.md) gives two formulas that write zeta(3) purely as sums over powers of the golden ratio with no extra pieces left over after a 2025 paper reported that a search for such a formula did not succeed and the page adds shorter formulas for zeta(5) and zeta(7).
- [long-formulas.md](long-formulas.md) prints four long formulas for zeta(5) and zeta(7) and zeta(9) with every whole-number coefficient written out plus three near-miss coincidences and a list of searches that found nothing.
- [other-bases.md](other-bases.md) tests 23 different base numbers and finds a formula for zeta(5) with no extra pieces in the base near 0.7549 (1 divided by the plastic ratio) plus formulas for zeta(3) with no extra pieces in five more bases and a fast formula for zeta(5) in the base 2 - sqrt(3).
- [salem-bases.md](salem-bases.md) finds formulas with no extra pieces for zeta(7) and zeta(9) in a base near 0.7136 and for zeta(11) and zeta(13) and zeta(15) in a base near 0.8501 and counts how many independent formulas five such bases give.
- [salem-formulas.txt](salem-formulas.txt) is a plain text file that holds every formula from the page salem-bases.md plus the formulas that are too long to print on that page.
