# When did the Turkish para stop being money (a partial answer)

One AI model (Claude) did the search in a single chat on 12 November 2026 and no other person has checked the sources yet which means the answer on this page is an unconfirmed best guess.

## The question

Somebody asked on the Wikipedia Reference Desk (Humanities) when the Turkish para was formally abolished as a subdivision of the kuruş (40 para made 1 kuruş and 100 kuruş made 1 lira).
The helpers on the board got as far as a report that the last para coin was minted in 1942 and the formal ending stayed unconfirmed.

## What the records show

1. The Turkish State Mint lists the 10 para coin as entering circulation in 1940 and leaving circulation in 1950. The 10 para coin was the last para coin issued in Turkey so 1950 is the year the last para coin stopped being legal money. Source: https://www.darphane.gov.tr/cumhuriyet-donemi-tedavul-paralari

2. Turkish newspapers in 1941 and 1942 printed a Finance Ministry notice that withdrew the old nickel 5 para and 10 para and 20 para coins because enough scalloped 1 kuruş coins and bronze 10 para coins had been struck. The notice gave one more year for handing the old coins in at government cash offices and Central Bank branches. The scans are garbled around the dates so the cutoff date of 1 July 1942 is a best reading from three scans. Source: https://www.gastearsivi.com/gazete/son_posta/1942-06-10/8

3. A Turkish parliament document from the late 1960s shows the coin law (law number 6797) being amended with a list of permitted coins that runs from 2.5 lira down to 1 kuruş. No para coin could be legally struck under that list because the smallest permitted coin was 1 kuruş. Source: https://www5.tbmm.gov.tr/tutanaklar/TUTANAK/MM__/d03/c004/mm__03004072ss0102.pdf

## Best-guess answer

One possibility that fits all three records is that Turkish lawmakers never passed a single act abolishing the para and that the para ended in steps: the old nickel para coins stopped circulating in mid-1942 and the last para coin was withdrawn in 1950 and later coin law allowed no coin below 1 kuruş.

## What is still open

The Turkish Official Gazette archive refuses automated readers so the withdrawal notice for the bronze 10 para coin (probably printed in 1949 or 1950) was not read.
A person who opens the Official Gazette issues from 1949 and 1950 could confirm the exact day and could check for an explicit abolition sentence.
