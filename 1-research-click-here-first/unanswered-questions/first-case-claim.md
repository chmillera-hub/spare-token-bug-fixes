# Checking a "first reported case" claim in a medical database (PubMed)

One AI model (Claude) did the search in a single chat on 12 November 2026 and no other person has checked the sources yet which means the result on this page is unconfirmed.

## The question

A 2026 letter in the journal Dermatology Reports (PubMed number 42639963) has the title "New-onset alopecia areata during calcitonin gene-related peptide inhibitor therapy: first reported case".
Alopecia areata means patchy hair loss caused by the immune system and calcitonin gene-related peptide inhibitors are migraine prevention drugs such as erenumab and galcanezumab and fremanezumab.
The question tested was whether patchy immune hair loss had already been recorded with the migraine drugs before the 2026 letter.

## What the records show

1. A 2023 review in the journal Cephalalgia (PubMed number 36739513) searched the United States drug side effect database and noted alopecia areata in 4 reports for the migraine antibody drugs. Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/03331024221143538

2. A hair clinic page from 2023 states that alopecia areata occurred in 0.01% to 0.1% of patients in the large trials of galcanezumab. The clinic page is a secondhand source and the trial documents were not read. Source: https://donovanmedical.com/qow-posts/gepants-aa

3. No earlier detailed write-up of one patient with alopecia areata on the migraine drugs turned up in the search.

## Best-guess answer

The 2026 letter could be the first detailed published description of one patient because no earlier detailed description turned up.
The 2026 letter is not the first record of alopecia areata with the migraine drugs because four database reports were already counted in a 2023 review.
