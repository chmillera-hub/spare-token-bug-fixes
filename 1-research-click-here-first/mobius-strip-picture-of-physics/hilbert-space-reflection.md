# How Hilbert space could be expressed in the Mobius strip picture

> **About this page:** One AI model (Claude) wrote this page on 27 November 2026 after the author of this repository asked how Hilbert space might be expressed in the ribbon picture of the main document in this folder. The parts about whole-number labels and sliding quantities were added the same day after the author asked which variables of a Mobius strip are discrete and which are continuous. The page holds the chatbot's reflections and is a way of imagining. The page is not tested physics and no other person has reviewed the page.

## What Hilbert space is in plain words

Hilbert space is the bookkeeping that quantum physics uses for everything a particle could be doing at once. The state of a particle is pictured as one arrow in a space that has one direction for each basic possibility. The lengths of the arrow along those directions say how much of each possibility is present.

The main document never uses the name Hilbert space and yet the main document already holds most of the pieces. Each part below takes one piece of Hilbert space and points to the part of the ribbon picture that could express that piece.

## 1. The arrow could be the whole ripple pattern on a ribbon

The part of the main document about superposition says one ribbon can carry many ripples stacked on top of each other. Every ripple pattern on a closed loop can be built by adding pure notes in different amounts which is proven mathematics about waves on a loop. So the basic directions of Hilbert space could be the pure notes of the ribbon and the arrow could be the list of how much of each note the ribbon carries.

## 2. The two-lap rule is the most exact match

In Hilbert space the arrow for an electron turns into the negative of the same arrow after one full turn and returns to the start after two full turns. The main document describes an ant that walks one lap around a Mobius strip and arrives upside down and needs a second lap to arrive right side up. The upside down ant is that same sign flip.

A wave living on a Mobius strip has to flip sign after one lap for the same reason and the flip forces the notes of the wave to step in halves. This match is a mathematical one and not a loose comparison because the waves that fit on a Mobius strip are exactly the waves that change sign after one lap.

## 3. The corkscrew could carry the two-part numbers

Each amount in Hilbert space is a number with two parts which are a size and a position around a circle. The main document pictures light as a corkscrew with a tip that keeps circling. The width of the corkscrew could be the size and the place of the tip on the circle could be the second part.

## 4. A measurement could be a shadow

A measurement in Hilbert space takes the shadow of the arrow along one chosen direction. The main document says a measurement works like a prism that lets one ripple through. The main document also says a Mobius strip shows a different outline from each viewing direction. The page [shadow-finding.md](shadow-finding.md) in this folder counted 37 different shadows of a Mobius strip. So the idea that what a person finds depends on which direction the person asks from is already in this folder.

## 5. The half-angle page supplies the chance rule for one spin

Hilbert space says the chance of a result is the squared length of the shadow. For the spin of one particle that rule becomes the square of the cosine of half the angle between two directions. The page [half-angle-finding.md](half-angle-finding.md) in this folder got that exact rate from a randomly pointing arrow in three dimensions.

The same page found that a random point on a circle gives the wrong rate. That result fits Hilbert space because the states of one spin map onto the surface of a ball and not onto a circle.

## 6. The order of the questions

In Hilbert space two measurements can give different results depending on which measurement is done first. The main document describes a paper Mobius strip that has two sides to the eye and one side to a finger tracing the surface. Each method of checking gives a true answer that the other method cannot give which is a start on the idea that the order and the kind of question matter.

## The whole-number labels and the sliding quantities of a Mobius strip

The author of this repository asked which variables of a Mobius strip are discrete (whole-number labels) and which are continuous (sliding quantities) and how the two kinds could stand for the complexity of Hilbert space. The author named the first examples: the spin rate and the length and the three-dimensional orientation as continuous variables and the direction of spin and the number of half twists as discrete variables. The lists below start from those examples.

**Discrete variables (whole-number labels)**

- The number of half twists is 1 or 2 or 3 and onward because a closed band cannot hold a fraction of a half twist.
- The twist count is odd or even. An odd count gives a one-sided band and an even count gives a two-sided band so the character of the band flips with each added half twist.
- The handedness of the twist is left or right.
- The sense of spinning around a chosen axis is one way or the other way.
- The knot type is an unknotted loop or a trefoil or a more tangled knot because a knot cannot change into another knot without a cut.
- The number of separate loops and the number of times two loops link through each other are whole numbers.
- The wave count of a ripple is a whole number on a plain closed loop. On a Mobius strip the ripple has to flip sign after one lap so the counts step in halves such as 1/2 and 3/2 and 5/2.

**Continuous variables (sliding quantities)**

- The length of the strip runs from the minimum of about 1.732 times the width up to any larger length.
- The width of the strip.
- The spin rate runs from zero up to the rate where the edge of the strip would be moving at the speed of light.
- The position of the strip in space takes three numbers.
- The orientation of the strip takes three angles because two angles say which way the loop faces and a third angle says how far the strip is turned around that facing direction.
- The speed and direction of travel take three numbers.
- The place where the twist sits along the loop and how far the twist is spread out.
- The bending shape of the strip. A floppy ribbon can bend in endlessly many ways so the shape alone takes an endless list of sliding numbers.
- The size and the timing of each ripple on the strip which is two sliding numbers for every ripple the strip carries.
- The tension in the strip.

## How the two kinds of variable could map onto Hilbert space

A discrete variable with a set number of choices gives that many basic directions. Left or right handedness gives 2 directions which is the size of the Hilbert space for one spin.

A continuous variable gives endlessly many basic directions because every position or every angle counts as a separate possibility. That is why the Hilbert space for where a particle is has endless directions while the Hilbert space for spin has 2.

Combining variables multiplies the directions. A strip with 2 choices of handedness and 2 senses of spinning has 4 basic directions and not 2 + 2.

**The amounts are continuous even for a discrete variable.** Hilbert space lets a state be part left and part right at once with a sliding amount of each. So a variable with 2 choices still leads to a continuous family of states and for 2 choices that family has the shape of the surface of a ball. A strip that is simply left or right falls short at that point. The stacked ripples from the main document would have to carry the "part of each" information.

**A closed-up continuous variable produces discrete notes.** The position around a closed loop is a continuous variable and yet the ripples that fit on the loop have whole-number wave counts. The orientation of an object in three dimensions is continuous and yet the notes of a turning object step in fixed amounts which is where the fixed steps of spin arise in standard physics. A continuous variable that closes back on itself is the standard source of discreteness in quantum physics and the half twist is what shifts the steps to halves.

**A caution about spin rate and orientation.** Quantum physics does not allow the orientation and the spin rate to both be exact labels at once in the same way that position and speed cannot both be exact. So the Hilbert space would be built over the orientation alone or over the spin rate alone and the other one shows up as the notes. In that view the spin rate would not be a smooth dial from zero to the speed of light because a closed-up orientation forces the spin rate into steps.

**How much complexity one strip could hold.** One strip with all the variables above already gives endless basic directions mostly from the bending shape and the ripples. The gap named in the next part still stands because many particles need the directions multiplied together and separate strips in ordinary space give variables that add.

## Where the ribbon picture falls short

**Many particles.** The Hilbert space for two particles is the product of the two separate spaces and not the sum. So 10 spins need 1024 directions and 300 spins need more directions than there are atoms in the visible universe. Ripples on a ribbon sitting in ordinary space are one pattern over one surface and that kind of pattern grows by adding. The single stretched ribbon in the part of the main document about entanglement handles one pair. The chatbot does not see how the ribbon picture reaches the multiplying growth for many particles. This is the largest gap and standard physics has no easy picture for the multiplying growth either.

**The squared rule in general.** The half-angle page covers one spin measured along a direction. Hilbert space applies the squared rule to every kind of measurement.

**The circling tip.** Part 3 above uses a circle for the second part of each number. The half-angle page shows that a circle alone gives the wrong rate. So the ribbon picture would need to say how the circling tip and the three-dimensional tumbling fit together.

## One sentence that sums up the expression

Hilbert space could be the collection of every ripple pattern a ribbon complex can carry where a state is one pattern and a measurement is a shadow of the pattern and the half twist is the reason the pattern flips sign after one lap.
