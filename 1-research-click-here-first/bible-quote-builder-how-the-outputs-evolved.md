# How the outputs of the Bible quote builder evolved step by step

This page shows one sample output from each stage of the Bible quote builder so that a visitor can watch the outputs change from noise into readable paragraphs.
The Bible quote builder is a working name for an experiment where ten copying agents paste pieces of the World English Bible together while a simple reader favors strings that match the Bible and an AI model (Claude) then joins the result by hand under rules that Charles set one at a time.
Every sample comes from the words of Jesus in the four gospels unless the line says otherwise because following one selection makes the change from stage to stage easy to see.
Added or changed words carry stars from stage 4 onward and the scores use seven sentence rules from the page named writing-rules-for-clear-chatbot-replies in this folder with a top score of 14.
The AI model graded outputs that the AI model wrote which means the scores are a rough guide and not an outside measurement.

Warning about changed wording: the later samples are not exact Bible quotes because the rules allow added words and swapped pronouns and replaced names and added punctuation and the name "yahweh" is always replaced by the word "god" which means a reader who wants the exact wording needs to open the World English Bible itself.

## Stage 1: agents copy single letters

- The agents changed one letter at a time and the reader gave credit for getting close to a short Bible quote.
- The method could finish short quotes such as "love your enemies" and became far too slow for large quote lists (about 100 to 500 pastes per second).
- No sample is shown because the strings were mostly half-built words.

## Stage 2: agents copy whole words

- Sample: "when you pray / as in heaven / but sinners to repentance / word of the kingdom / but know this / and not perceive / given to god / and to return / distress in the land / but you dont believe"
- Each piece between the slashes is a real Bible fragment of three or four words and the pieces have nothing to do with each other because the reader only checked whether each piece was a real quote.

## Stage 3: agents copy key phrases of about four words

- Sample of rebuilt stretches: "before you you are the salt of the earth" and "good and faithful servant you have been faithful" and "and they have believed that you sent me i made known to them your name"
- The reader now gave a point for every pair of neighboring phrases that are also neighbors in the Bible so the agents rebuilt true stretches of up to about 25 words.

## Stage 4: the stretches are joined by hand with generic words only (6 of 14)

- for they shall inherit the earth blessed are those who have been persecuted *here* before you most certainly i tell you if you have faith with him then he will sit on the throne of his glory *and those* who hate you bless those *others* on the earth i wish it were already kindled *there* in the marketplaces *and not* the best seats in the synagogues *for those* with you how long will i bear with you for everyone *here* and traded with them i tell you and much more *than that*
- Only connecting words and pointing words were allowed and the stretches stayed in Bible order which left one long run-on sentence.

## Stage 5: free order and sentence breaks and a reason in every sentence (10 of 14)

- with you how long will i bear with you for everyone *there* in the marketplaces *and in* the best seats in the synagogues *because those* and traded with them i tell you and much more. *those* who hate you bless those *others* for they shall inherit the earth blessed are those who have been persecuted *here* before you. most certainly i tell you if you have faith with him then he will sit on the throne of his glory *over all those*. on the earth i wish it were already kindled *for all of those here*.
- Rearranging the stretches let every sentence carry a claim and a reason.

## Stage 6: naming words that already appear in the string may be reused (12 of 14)

- with you how long will i bear with you for everyone *there* in the marketplaces *and in* the best seats in the synagogues *because those in the synagogues* and traded with them i tell you and much more. *those* who hate you bless those *in the marketplaces* for they shall inherit the earth blessed are those who have been persecuted *on the earth* before you. most certainly i tell you if you have faith with him then he will sit on the throne of his glory *over the earth*. on the earth i wish it were already kindled *this faith in all of you here*.
- Phrases such as "those in the synagogues" now say who a sentence is about.

## Stage 7: special naming words and action words and describing words may fill the gaps (14 of 14)

- with you how long will i *jesus* bear with you for everyone in the marketplaces *and in* the best seats in the synagogues *made the house of god a house of the world* and traded with them i tell you and much more. *the father sent love to those* who hate you *so* bless those *in the marketplaces* for they shall inherit the earth blessed are those who have been persecuted *for the kingdom* before you. most certainly i tell you if you have faith with him *the christ* then he will sit on the throne of his glory *which the father made great in heaven*. on the earth i wish it were already kindled *the good seed of love that the father sent to the world*.
- Each selection received about 12 of its most used naming words and action words and describing words and an action word lets a gap hold a whole small claim such as "the father sent love to those".

## Stage 8: punctuation and pronoun deletion and grammar changes and the speaker rule (14 of 14)

- "with you, how long will i bear with you? for everyone in the marketplaces, *and in* the best seats in the synagogues, *made the house of god a house of the world* and traded with them, i tell you, and much more. *the father sent love to those* who hate you, *so* bless those *in the marketplaces*, for they shall inherit the earth. blessed are those who have been persecuted *for the kingdom* before you. most certainly i tell you, if you have faith with *the son of man*, then he will sit on the throne of his glory, *which the father made great in heaven*. on the earth, i wish it were already kindled: *the good seed of love that the father sent to the world*."
- Periods and commas and question marks and quote marks were added and a pronoun beside an added name may be deleted and a word may change form to keep the grammar correct and every words of Jesus passage is read as spoken by Christ which is why "the son of man" stands where "jesus" and "the christ" stood in stage 7.

## Stage 9: any pronoun may be swapped for a naming word and rarely used names become generic words (13 of 14 on a fresh string)

- "*the man who hears the father* who is in heaven, *that man* is my brother. *the lord says to the man,* 'well done, good and faithful servant,' and give *the kingdom* to *the servant* who has the ten talents. *the man who loves the world more* than me is not worthy of me, and *the man* who doesnt have *love*, even that which he has will be taken away from him. *the father had a son, and the father* sent *the son* last to *the farmers*, saying, '*the farmers* will respect my son.' but the farmers *did not receive the son*. *it will be right* for *two foreign cities* in the judgment with this generation, *because this generation saw the son and did not believe*. *love your father* and your mother; you still lack one thing: *give to the little ones and enter the kingdom*."
- Pronouns may also be added or deleted anywhere in every selection when the change helps a reader.
- The source stretch read "he sent him last to them saying they will respect my son" and now names the father and the son and the farmers and the city names "tyre and sidon" became "two foreign cities".

## Side experiment: John 10 dealt into four random piles

- The 201 key phrases of John chapter 10 were dealt at random into four piles and the AI model built one paragraph from every phrase in each pile.
- Each pile came out as a small version of the whole chapter because the chapter repeats its main ideas and about one word in three had to be added.
- Three full remixes (twelve paragraphs) are on the page named john-10-remixes-from-four-random-piles in this folder.

## Side experiment: sixteen random phrases kept in a random order

- Sixteen key phrases were drawn at random from the ten whole books of the Charles selection and shuffled three times and the AI model connected the phrases in the exact shuffled order with no sorting.
- Each of the three passages reads as a short connected scene and about half of the words in each passage had to be added and the three passages tell three different stories.
- The three passages are on the page named bible-key-phrase-evolution in this folder in the section about sixteen random phrases.

## Stage 11: every word different and an open vocabulary on random phrases in a random order

Claude selection (12 of 14 with 58 different words out of 58 words) from the eight phrases in this drawn order: "before you" and "that he" and "whoever shall put away his wife" and "if only you god" and "ask of the father" and "for they think" and "than these is of the evil one" and "dont sound a trumpet":

- *mercy walks* before *every traveler, so* that he *who stumbles finds welcome.* whoever shall put away his wife *must first weigh her sorrow.* if only you, god, *would soften hard hearts!* ask of *your* father *plainly,* for *proud people* think *louder prayers win; anything showier* than these *quiet words* is *from* the evil one. dont sound a trumpet.

- "*visit your neighbor* and tell him, 'friend, *comfort comes at an hour* that you *cannot* expect; therefore dont be afraid.' *if dread speaks, reply,* 'get behind me, satan.' from one end of *earth people mourn,* sitting in sackcloth; *carry light with*in yourselves, *because whoever does so* doesnt stumble."

- Eight phrases were drawn at random from each selection and kept in a random order and the AI model could add almost any English word while keeping every word in the passage different.

## What the ten stages show together

- The scores for one words of Jesus string rose from 6 to 10 to 12 to 14 as the rules widened which means each new rule removed one kind of obstacle for a reader.
- The largest jumps came from free ordering with sentence breaks (6 to 10) and from allowing action words in the gaps (13 to 14 with a much fuller first sentence).
- Every wider rule also moved more of the meaning from the agents to the AI model because the agents only supply true Bible fragments and the AI model chooses the order and the added words and who each pronoun points at.
- The later paragraphs are therefore best read as short compositions built around real Bible fragments and not as quotations or as teaching from the Bible.
- The live programs run stage 3 with single joining words added and do not perform stages 4 to 10 because the programs have no test of meaning or grammar.
