# Kindness games

This folder holds small computer tournaments that test how a player who always makes kind moves can do well among players who make harmful moves. An AI chatbot (Claude) ran the tournaments and wrote the pages on 15 November 2026 and no other person has rechecked the pages.

- [love-your-enemies-report.md](love-your-enemies-report.md) reports four tests of surroundings that help kindness survive which are an exit and a sample game at one tenth of the stakes and a buffer that shows the other player's move in advance and a visible record that lets a player decline a match. The report ends with a reflection on how each rule of the game reads like a stage of human trust such as coffee before sharing an apartment.
