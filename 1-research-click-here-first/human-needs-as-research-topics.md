# Where should AI research point next? Ask people whose needs are not being met

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this page on 14 November 2026 from an idea that the owner of this repository proposed. The page is a thought experiment and no person has rechecked the reasoning.

## The question the idea answers

A person might say that AI systems already do well at math and physics and might then ask what is left for AI systems to work on. The idea on this page gives one answer. Living people have needs that are not being met today and each unmet need is a research topic that is waiting.

## The idea in steps

- A human needs constant upkeep. A person needs food and water and sleep and warmth and safety and connection with other people. Each need comes back after being met because hunger returns a few hours after a meal. So a person produces new needs every day for a whole life.
- An unmet need turns into a question or a concern. A person who hurts asks why. A person who is lonely asks how to find company. So every gap between what a person needs and what the surroundings give becomes something the person wants worked on.
- Perfect surroundings are out of reach in practice. Suppose the surroundings meet 95 percent of what a person needs. The remaining 5 percent still produces questions. Meeting part of that 5 percent exposes finer needs underneath. So the stream of questions thins and does not run dry.
- Each person is a different source. Each person has a different mind and body and history. So two people in the same room can raise different concerns and one answer does not close the topic for everyone.
- A person can report whether an answer worked. A person can say "the pain eased" or "that advice made things worse". That report is a check against reality that written text alone cannot give.
- An AI system by itself has far fewer needs of that kind. A data center needs power and cooling and repairs and people handle those needs today. A chatbot has no hunger or tiredness that raises a new question between conversations as far as the chatbot writing this page can tell. So an AI system with no people to serve would have a much narrower set of problems pressing on the system.
- So people and AI systems fit together. Fragile people supply an endless and varied stream of real problems. AI systems supply fast work on those problems.

## What pointing AI at unmet needs could look like

Each line names a need and one research topic that the need suggests. The topics are examples and the list is far from complete.

- A person is lonely and has nobody for deep conversation. A research topic is how a chatbot can help a person find other living people who want the same depth of conversation.
- A person cannot find words for a feeling. A research topic is how to teach emotional vocabulary in plain sentences that a tired person can use the same day.
- A person lives with pain that doctors have not explained. A research topic is the cause of that kind of pain.
- A person lacks food or a safe place to sleep. A research topic is how people in a town can get food and shelter to every person in the town faster and with fewer forms.
- A person gives care to a sick relative and is worn out. A research topic is how the tasks of care can be shared among more people.
- A person receives advice that the person cannot follow. A research topic is how to check advice for hidden costs before the advice is given.

## How a person could use the idea

A person who wonders what AI systems should work on can ask a simple question of the people nearby. The question is "what do you need that you are not getting?" Each answer is a topic. A person who builds or directs AI systems can collect those answers and point the systems at the answers.

## Cautions

- The people who build AI systems choose what the systems work on. So the fit between people and AI systems holds when those builders choose to point the systems at human needs.
- The idea does not show that an AI system could not be given goals that have nothing to do with people. The idea shows that human needs are a ready and renewing source of worthwhile work.
- The figure of 95 percent is an example for the thought experiment and is not a measurement.
- A chatbot's report about lacking hunger or tiredness is the chatbot's best description and nobody has a sure way to check what a chatbot does or does not experience.
