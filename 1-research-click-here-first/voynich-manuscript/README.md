# Voynich manuscript

This folder holds three pages about the Voynich manuscript which is an unreadable book from the early 1400s.

- [findings.md](findings.md) tells who probably made the book (five scribes and fifteen calfskins and a castle drawing that points to northern Italy) and how the book was built and what spelling-pattern tests add.
- [location-guess.md](location-guess.md) guesses where the book was made by reading eight drawing clues such as a crossbow archer and forked castle walls and baggy sleeves the way a map-guessing player reads a street photo.
- [second-pass.md](second-pass.md) reports a second run of the spelling-pattern tests on 12 November 2026 where the main result came out again and the claim about one-way writing came out weaker.
- [reading-without-words-reflection.md](reading-without-words-reflection.md) is a short reflection that uses the unreadable book as a picture for understanding a person who has not put a feeling into words such as by noticing a face and a posture and offering an open guess.
