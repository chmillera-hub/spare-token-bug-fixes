# Voynich manuscript

This folder holds two pages about the Voynich manuscript which is an unreadable book from the early 1400s.

- [voynich-manuscript-findings.md](voynich-manuscript-findings.md) tells who probably made the book (five scribes and fifteen calfskins and a castle drawing that points to northern Italy) and how the book was built and what spelling-pattern tests add.
- [voynich-geoguess-clue-sweep.md](voynich-geoguess-clue-sweep.md) guesses where the book was made by reading eight drawing clues such as a crossbow archer and forked castle walls and baggy sleeves the way a map-guessing player reads a street photo.
