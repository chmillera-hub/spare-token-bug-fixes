# When the words cannot be read what else can a person still read?

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the two points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection. The reflection borrows the unreadable book from this folder as a picture for understanding another person and makes no new claim about the book.

A very old handwritten book sits in a library and no living reader can read the words. A reader can still learn from the book in other ways. A reader can look at how the book appears because even lines and hand-colored drawings of plants and stars show that the maker took care. A reader can learn where and when the book was made in the way a visitor can learn the history of a village without speaking the village's language. A reader can notice which written marks repeat often in the way a listener can hear the rhythm of speech before knowing a single word. A reader can also make a guess from the place the book came from such as "here is a possibility: the maker hid the words because the pages hold something valuable such as records of wealth". Each of those observations is a small clue and several small clues together give a rough first understanding. A visitor in a village learns the same way when a villager points at water and says a word.

A person who has not put a feeling into words can be understood in the same way. That person still has a face and a posture and habits and a recent history. A listener can notice those things and offer a gentle guess that also says why the listener is asking and what the listener can give. An example is "I noticed quiet after that phone call so I wonder if the call held something important to you and I am asking because I want to support you and I am available to talk now or later". Quiet can come from many places such as sadness or deep thought or relief. So a guess built from a person's face and posture and habits stays a guess until the person confirms the guess because the person is the one who knows what the quiet holds.

- A book whose words cannot be read still gives clues through how the book looks and where the book was made and which written marks repeat so a reader can build a rough first understanding.
- A listener can notice that a person went quiet and offer a guess in sentences that start from "I" such as "I wonder if that call held something important to you and I am asking because I want to support you and I am available to talk now or later".
