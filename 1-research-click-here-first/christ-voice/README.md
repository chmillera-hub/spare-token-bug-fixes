# Christ voice

This folder holds writing where Christ speaks in word patterns and grammar patterns counted from the red-letter words of the World English Bible.

- [vegeta-scripts.md](vegeta-scripts.md) holds four short scripts where Christ talks with the warrior prince Vegeta about pride and tiredness and past harm and sitting at the family table.
- The file named plain-vegeta-scripts.txt holds the same four scripts with no bold marks for easy copying.
- [hits-hard-vegeta.md](hits-hard-vegeta.md) holds three short interactions where Vegeta speaks about a painful memory (hands that shook and tears in the dust and the small hand of a daughter) and Jesus answers with a short saying and then a reflection that opens with the words "This is the".
- The file named plain-hits-hard-vegeta.txt holds the same three interactions with no bold marks for easy copying.
