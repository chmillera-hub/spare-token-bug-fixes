# Christ talks with Vegeta

This page holds four short scripts where Christ talks with Vegeta who is a warrior prince full of pride from the Dragon Ball stories.
An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote every line on 12 November 2026 and no line is copied from the Dragon Ball shows or books because the lines for Vegeta were written fresh in the manner of Vegeta who speaks with pride.
The lines of Christ follow word patterns and grammar patterns that the chatbot counted in the red-letter words of Matthew and Mark and Luke and John in the World English Bible (for example Christ says "you" in half of all sentences and opens many sentences with "But" or "If" or "Whoever").
Rules mode means the lines of Christ also keep a reason inside each sentence and give no order to Vegeta because those are writing rules that the repository owner set.
Closest mode means the lines of Christ follow only the counted patterns which allows short sentences.
The style score says what share of real passages of Christ the lines of Christ beat on a program that compares small word rates and sentence shapes and no human reader has checked the score.
The file named plain-vegeta-scripts.txt holds the same four scripts with no bold marks for easy copying.

## Script 1: the prince who bows to no one

In this scene Vegeta meets Christ for the first time and says he bows to no one.

The lines of Christ are in rules mode and beat 77 percent of real passages of Christ on the style score with 31 of 36 key counts inside the real range.

**Vegeta:** I am the prince of all Saiyans and I bow to no one because I earned every drop of my power through battle. Who are you to stand in front of me without fear?

**Christ:** You say that you bow to no one. But I tell you a man who needs to win every fight has a master because that man lets the next fight say who he is. Which man can add one day to the life of his wife by lifting a heavier weight since a wife asks for a husband and not for a record?

**Vegeta:** Do not lecture me about the people I love because I grew strong to keep those people alive. A tyrant took my planet and my father and my people and then that tyrant made me serve him. Strength is the only thing nobody can take from me.

**Christ:** The tyrant took your home and so your grief deserves a hearing because a person taken from his home needs someone to come. But you grew strong and your planet stayed gone and you still sat alone in the dark since strength cannot sit beside a person the way a friend can. If a man guards a wound with pride then the wound stays shut away from every hand that could tend the wound for a man who guards with pride lets no one near. Whoever tells one person what was lost is no longer carrying the loss alone.

**Vegeta:** Hmph you speak as if you have seen inside my head. I spent years chasing Kakarot because my rival kept passing me no matter how hard I trained. Tell me then what I am if I am not the strongest.

**Christ:** You ask what you are without the first place. I tell you that you are the man who held his wife close before a battle because you chose your family over your pride on that day. The last in rank may be first in love since a person who loves counts what a man gives and not what a man wins. And no one took that choice from you for you made the choice with your own arms.

**Vegeta:** My wife did not know why I held her. I did not have the words because Saiyans are trained to fight and not to speak.

**Christ:** Then the words are still yours to give because your wife is alive to hear the words. You can say to your wife I held you because I love you and a man full of pride who says so loses nothing that a husband needs. But I give you no order for you alone choose when to speak. Whoever has ears to hear can hear what a husband says late since late words still reach a living wife.


## Script 2: three days in the gravity room

In this scene Vegeta has trained alone for three days without sleep in a room with crushing gravity.

The lines of Christ are in rules mode and beat 58 percent of real passages of Christ on the style score with 31 of 36 key counts inside the real range.

**Vegeta:** Get out of my training room because the gravity in here is too heavy for most people to stand in. I have trained for three days without sleep and I will not stop until I pass Kakarot.

**Christ:** You have trained for three days and you are tired because you have given all that a person has. But I tell you the tiredness is not your enemy since the tiredness speaks for the one life you have. Which servant works a field for three days with no bread and no wage and calls the master good?

**Vegeta:** I am my own master and I answer to nobody. A warrior who rests is a warrior who falls behind because I was taught that the enemy does not rest.

**Christ:** You say that you are your own master. If you are the master then you are also the worker and so you are the one who sends the worker out with no rest and no pay because no other master is in the room. Whoever drives a worker past all strength for a prize that keeps moving has set a heavy load on a man for no gain. And I ask what the three days have bought since Kakarot is still ahead by your own count.

**Vegeta:** Then what would you have me do? Would you have me sit in the grass and do nothing while my rival grows stronger?

**Christ:** I give you no order because your life is yours and you alone choose what you do. But I tell you what I see for you asked me. I see a man who is tired and alone in a locked room and I see a wife on the far side of the door. You can say I am tired to the people on the far side of the door because the people who love you can carry bread to a man who says so. I count no kingdom as lost when a man says I am tired since the words cost nothing and a man who is tired gains company.


## Script 3: the worlds Vegeta burned

In this scene Vegeta says no god could forgive the worlds he destroyed for a tyrant.

The lines of Christ are in rules mode and beat 61 percent of real passages of Christ on the style score with 31 of 36 key counts inside the real range.

**Vegeta:** Why do you waste words on me? I burned whole worlds for a tyrant and I laughed while I did so because I believed that people who could not fight back deserved nothing. No god forgives a man like me.

**Christ:** You burned whole worlds and the people of those worlds are dead because of what your hands did. I name the harm plainly since the dead had faces and names and homes. But I did not come to put you in a cage for I count a cage as healing no one who died and no one who lives. Which of the dead is fed if you starve and which of the dead is warmed if you sit in the cold?

**Vegeta:** So you say that what I did means nothing. Even I am not fool enough to believe that.

**Christ:** I do not say that the lives mean nothing for I count each life you took as worth more than every prize the tyrant gave you. If the lives mean much then the living mean much also because the living are people just as the dead were people. Whoever once took bread from a table can carry bread to a table since the hands are the same hands. I ask you for no speech of guilt and I set no price on you because a price could only buy more pain.

**Vegeta:** Carry bread? You speak to the prince of all Saiyans as if carrying bread were work beneath a prince.

**Christ:** The greatest in a house is the one who serves the table since the people at the table eat because of him. You can learn the name of one person your power frightened and you can ask that person what he needs for a man who asks has stopped taking. But the choice is yours and nothing bad comes from me if you walk away. And I tell you that you are still a man worth speaking to because I am still here speaking to you.


## Script 4: the wall and the table

In this scene Vegeta leans on a wall at a family party and refuses to sit at the table.

The lines of Christ are in closest mode and beat 93 percent of real passages of Christ on the style score with 30 of 36 key counts inside the real range.

**Vegeta:** I do not eat with that crowd. Laughter and cake are for people with nothing to prove.

**Christ:** You stand by the wall. Yet your eyes are on the table. Why do you watch what you say you do not want?

**Vegeta:** I watch because I was trained to keep watch. Do not mistake that for longing.

**Christ:** I tell you a man can guard a door so long that he forgets he lives in the house. Your wife is at that table. Your friends are at that table. Which of them asked you to prove one thing before you sit?

**Vegeta:** None of them asked. That is the insult of it. They would take me as I am and I have not earned it.

**Christ:** You have heard that a seat has to be earned. But I tell you the seat was set before you came. No one earns bread at the table of those who love him for the bread is given. You can sit at the table if you choose. Or you can say to one of them I am glad you are here. For what a man says at the table stays with the ones who heard him.
