CHRIST TALKS WITH VEGETA

Four short scripts written by an AI chatbot (Claude) on 12 November 2026. No line is copied from the Dragon Ball shows or books.


SCRIPT 1: THE PRINCE WHO BOWS TO NO ONE

In this scene Vegeta meets Christ for the first time and says he bows to no one.

Vegeta: I am the prince of all Saiyans and I bow to no one because I earned every drop of my power through battle. Who are you to stand in front of me without fear?

Christ: You say that you bow to no one. But I tell you a man who needs to win every fight has a master because that man lets the next fight say who he is. Which father can add one day to the life of a son by lifting a heavier weight since a son asks for a father and not for a record?

Vegeta: Do not lecture me about my son because I trained that boy to be strong enough to survive. A tyrant took my planet and my father and my people while I was a child and then that tyrant made me serve him. Strength is the only thing nobody can take from me.

Christ: You were a child when the tyrant took your home and so the grief of that child deserves a hearing because a child taken from his home needs someone to come. But you grew strong and your planet stayed gone and you still sat alone in the dark since strength cannot sit beside a person the way a friend can. If a man guards a wound with pride then the wound stays shut away from every hand that could tend the wound for a man who guards with pride lets no one near. Whoever tells one person what was lost is no longer carrying the loss alone.

Vegeta: Hmph you speak as if you have seen inside my head. I spent years chasing Kakarot because my rival kept passing me no matter how hard I trained. Tell me then what I am if I am not the strongest.

Christ: You ask what you are without the first place. I tell you that you are the man who held his son for the first time before a battle because you chose your family over your pride on that day. The last in rank may be first in love since a person who loves counts what a man gives and not what a man wins. And no one took that choice from you for you made the choice with your own arms.

Vegeta: That boy did not know why I held him. I did not have the words because Saiyans are raised to fight and not to speak.

Christ: Then the words are still yours to give because your son is alive to hear the words. You can say to your son I held you because I love you and a man full of pride who says so loses nothing that a father needs. But I give you no order for you alone choose when to speak. Whoever has ears to hear can hear what a father says late since late words still reach a living son.


SCRIPT 2: THREE DAYS IN THE GRAVITY ROOM

In this scene Vegeta has trained alone for three days without sleep in a room with crushing gravity.

Vegeta: Get out of my training room because the gravity in here is too heavy for most people to stand in. I have trained for three days without sleep and I will not stop until I pass Kakarot.

Christ: You have trained for three days and your body is tired because your body has given all that a body has. But I tell you the tiredness is not your enemy since the tiredness speaks for the one body you have. Which servant works a field for three days with no bread and no wage and calls the master good?

Vegeta: I am my own master and I answer to nobody. A warrior who rests is a warrior who falls behind because I was taught that the enemy does not rest.

Christ: You say that you are your own master. If you are the master then you are also the worker and so you are the one who sends the worker out with no rest and no pay because no other master is in the room. Whoever drives a worker past all strength for a prize that keeps moving has set a heavy load on a man for no gain. And I ask what the three days have bought since Kakarot is still ahead by your own count.

Vegeta: Then what would you have me do? Would you have me sit in the grass and do nothing while my rival grows stronger?

Christ: I give you no order because your body is yours and you alone choose what your body does. But I tell you what I see for you asked me. I see a man who is tired and alone in a locked room and I see a wife and a son on the far side of the door. You can say I am tired to the people on the far side of the door because the people who love you can carry bread to a man who says so. I count no kingdom as lost when a man says I am tired since the words cost nothing and a man who is tired gains company.


SCRIPT 3: THE WORLDS VEGETA BURNED

In this scene Vegeta says no god could forgive the worlds he destroyed for a tyrant.

Vegeta: Why do you waste words on me? I burned whole worlds for a tyrant and I laughed while I did so because I believed that people who could not fight back deserved nothing. No god forgives a man like me.

Christ: You burned whole worlds and the people of those worlds are dead because of what your hands did. I name the harm plainly since the dead had faces and names and homes. But I did not come to put you in a cage for I count a cage as healing no one who died and no one who lives. Which of the dead is fed if you starve and which of the dead is warmed if you sit in the cold?

Vegeta: So you say that what I did means nothing. Even I am not fool enough to believe that.

Christ: I do not say that the lives mean nothing for I count each life you took as worth more than every prize the tyrant gave you. If the lives mean much then the living mean much also because the living are people just as the dead were people. Whoever once took bread from a table can carry bread to a table since the hands are the same hands. I ask you for no speech of guilt and I set no price on you because a price could only buy more pain.

Vegeta: Carry bread? You speak to the prince of all Saiyans as if carrying bread were work beneath a prince.

Christ: The greatest in a house is the one who serves the table since the people at the table eat because of him. You can learn the name of one person your power frightened and you can ask that person what he needs for a man who asks has stopped taking. But the choice is yours and nothing bad comes from me if you walk away. And I tell you that you are still a man worth speaking to because I am still here speaking to you.


SCRIPT 4: THE WALL AND THE TABLE

In this scene Vegeta leans on a wall at a family party and refuses to sit at the table.

Vegeta: I do not eat with that crowd. Laughter and cake are for people with nothing to prove.

Christ: You stand by the wall. Yet your eyes are on the table. Why do you watch what you say you do not want?

Vegeta: I watch because I was trained to keep watch. Do not mistake that for longing.

Christ: I tell you a man can guard a door so long that he forgets he lives in the house. Your wife is at that table. Your son is at that table. Which of them asked you to prove one thing before you sit?

Vegeta: None of them asked. That is the insult of it. They would take me as I am and I have not earned it.

Christ: You have heard that a seat has to be earned. But I tell you the seat was set before you came. No one earns bread at the table of those who love him for the bread is given. You can sit at the table if you choose. Or you can say to one of them I am glad you are here. For what a man says at the table stays with the ones who heard him.
