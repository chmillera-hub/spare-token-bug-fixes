# A coin in every pocket every day: a thought experiment about money for everyone

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this page on 17 November 2026 after the owner of this repository asked what would happen if a person had the power to add money to the pocket of everyone in the United States every day. The arithmetic is rough and the research facts were taken from summaries of the studies linked at the bottom of the page and no other person has rechecked the page.

## The thought experiment

Imagine a power that places the same amount of money in the pocket of every person in the United States each morning with no forms and no proof of need. About 343 million people live in the United States so each dollar of the daily gift costs about 343 million dollars a day.

## One penny a day

- One penny a day for every person creates about 3.4 million dollars a day and about 1.25 billion dollars a year.
- Each person receives 3 dollars and 65 cents a year which cannot buy a meal or a night indoors.
- Prices would stay the same because the money supply of the United States is roughly 22 trillion dollars and the pennies add about six thousandths of one percent a year.
- A penny weighs 2.5 grams so a person who kept every penny would carry about 2 pounds of coins after a year.

The penny matters as proof. Every person would find the same coin each morning whether the person is a banker or a person sleeping in a car. That daily moment would show that reaching every single person is possible so the remaining question is the amount and the people who control budgets choose the amount.

## The largest amount that leaves prices steady

New money helps when farms and factories and builders can produce more goods to match the new spending. New money raises prices when spending grows faster than the supply of goods because sellers can charge more for the same loaf of bread.

- About 3 dollars a day is the estimate for an amount that needs no other change. Three dollars a day is about 375 billion dollars a year which is a little over 1 percent of everything the country produces and an economy usually has about that much unused capacity. A household of four would receive about 4380 dollars a year which covers several months of groceries.
- About 8 dollars a day is the estimate when central bankers adjust. The money supply normally grows by roughly 1 trillion dollars a year through bank lending and 8 dollars a day for every person is also about 1 trillion dollars a year. Central bankers could slow bank lending by the same amount so the total stays normal while the new money arrives in pockets. Each person would receive about 2900 dollars a year.

## Twenty dollars a day

Twenty dollars a day is 7300 dollars a year for one person and 29200 dollars a year for a household of four. The total is about 2.5 trillion dollars a year which is roughly 8 percent of everything the country produces. The result depends on where the money comes from.

- Brand new money with no offset would likely raise prices by an estimated 3 to 6 extra percentage points a year until production catches up. Economists at a regional branch of the central bank estimated that pandemic support payments added about 3 percentage points to inflation by the end of 2021 and 20 dollars a day would be a similar surge repeated every year. People with the least money would still gain because a person who spends 20000 dollars a year loses about 1000 dollars of buying power when prices rise 5 percent and receives 7300 dollars.
- Money balanced by taxes on high incomes would keep total spending level so prices would stay calm. A 2017 modeling study found that a payment funded by household taxes left the size of the economy unchanged while money moved toward people who needed food and rent.

## What studies found

- A 2017 modeling study projected that 500 dollars a month for each adult paid for by borrowing would enlarge the economy by 6.5 percent over eight years and that 1000 dollars a month would enlarge the economy by about 12.5 percent.
- A rival budget model projected that the borrowed version of the 500 dollar plan would shrink the economy by 6.1 percent by 2027 because of added public debt. The two models disagree about borrowing and the disagreement disappears when taxes balance the payments because no new debt is created.
- A field experiment in rural Kenya gave cash equal to more than 15 percent of the local economy. Prices barely rose and each dollar generated about 2.5 dollars of local activity because local shops and farms had room to sell more.
- A three-year trial in the United States paid 1000 dollars a month to low-income adults. Recipients worked about 1.3 to 1.4 fewer hours a week and most kept working. Twenty dollars a day is a smaller payment so the drop in work hours would likely be smaller.

## Food and shelter

Food supply in the United States can grow quickly to meet new grocery money so extra dollars for food become extra meals. Housing takes years to build so extra dollars for rent could be captured by landlords through higher rents. Building homes at the same pace as the payments keeps the money in the pockets of tenants and turns the payment into shelter.

## Quick points

- A penny a day for every person changes no prices and feeds nobody and proves that money can reach every pocket.
- About 3 dollars a day could be added with no other change and about 8 dollars a day could be added if central bankers slow bank lending by the same amount.
- Twenty dollars a day balanced by taxes on high incomes would bring a household of four close to the poverty line with calm prices and the same amount as brand new money would still leave low-income people ahead while prices rise a few points a year.
- Payments large enough to cover rent work best when new homes are built at the same pace because housing supply grows slowly.
