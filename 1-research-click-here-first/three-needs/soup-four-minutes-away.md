One fact from the systems page is absurd once said plainly. A hot meal can reach a parked car in most American cities within the hour and a clean bed sits empty tonight in a motel near most highway exits and both arrive the instant a card number is typed. So the country already runs one of the largest food and shelter delivery networks in human history around the clock and the network works smoothly for a person ordering a third dessert. A person whose hands went numb in a car last night is standing inside that same network like a ghost because drivers and cooks and desk clerks are all on shift and all waiting for a number on a screen. The emergency was never a shortage of soup because the soup is hot and four minutes away.

A wildfire evacuee can be placed in a stranger's guest room for free through a charity that many thousands of hosts signed up for while a person freezing in a car three blocks from those same guest rooms does not qualify because cold without a news camera is not on the list. Somebody wrote that list and somebody could add a line to that list on a Tuesday afternoon between meetings. Food benefits will pay for a raw chicken and refuse a bowl of hot soup in most states to a person who owns no stove because an official once typed a rule about food that is hot at the register and no official has untyped the rule since. Every locked door on the systems page has a living person holding the key and most of those people will eat a warm dinner tonight without once thinking about the key in their pocket.
