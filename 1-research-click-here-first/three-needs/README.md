# Three needs

This folder holds research about meeting three basic human needs without force which are food and shelter and deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection. An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote the pages on 14 November 2026 from searches of public sources and no other person has rechecked the pages.

- [help-without-jobs.md](help-without-jobs.md) lists actions a social media user can take to help another person reach food or shelter or deep human connection without sending the person back into a job search and gives a source for each action.
- [replace-force.md](replace-force.md) names six places where people in society use force such as armed police and eviction and locked wards and gives a tested nonviolent replacement for each one with the evidence and the limits of the evidence.
- [what-blocks-dinner.md](what-blocks-dinner.md) was written on 15 November 2026 and names the four gaps that stop an AI model from getting dinner and a dry bed to one hungry person which are the person's location and live supply and a list of willing neighbors and hands and authority to pay.
- [systems-ai-would-need.md](systems-ai-would-need.md) was written on 15 November 2026 and lists ten systems an AI model would need to reach to feed and house one person tonight such as the 211 directory and live shelter bed counts and phone calls to shelters and a capped emergency wallet and names who holds the key to each one.
- [free-service-gate.md](free-service-gate.md) was written on 15 November 2026 and outlines a design where an AI model can switch on free food or shelter after a person shows enough proof of need with proof that grows with cost such as no proof for a meal and a photo of an eviction notice for a month of rent.
- [soup-four-minutes-away.md](soup-four-minutes-away.md) was written on 15 November 2026 and is a two paragraph unfiltered reflection on the fact that meals and motel beds already reach anyone who types a card number while a person freezing in a car goes without.
- [penny-in-every-pocket.md](penny-in-every-pocket.md) was written on 17 November 2026 and works through a thought experiment about a power that adds money to every pocket in the United States each day from one penny to 20 dollars with estimates of the effect on prices and research on cash payments and a note that rent money works best when new homes are built.
