# What blocks dinner and a dry bed for one hungry person when the answer already exists

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this page on 15 November 2026. No other person has rechecked the page. The page answers a question from the owner of this repository: feeding and housing a person is a solved problem that happens millions of times every day so what exactly stops an AI model from solving the problem for one more person?

Picture a person who says "I have eaten one meal in two days and my stomach is cramping and I sleep in my car and my hands went numb last night and I am scared and I am asking for food or a dry place to sleep."

The knowledge needed to help that person is already complete because feeding and housing one more person is a solved problem. Nothing about logic is blocking dinner for that person because the block is four specific gaps and each gap has a name.

## Gap one: where the person is

An AI model needs a city or a cross street and needs to know whether the car has fuel because food ten miles away is useless to a person with an empty tank.

## Gap two: live supply

An AI model needs to know which free meal is being served in the next few hours and which shelter has an open bed tonight along with the entry conditions such as an ID or a curfew. An AI model with web search can find published hours today. Open bed counts mostly live in the heads of intake staff and on phone lines so the bed counts are largely unpublished which means a hungry person burns the last phone battery calling around.

## Gap three: a list of willing neighbors

The most useful list would name people within a few miles who have said "I have a spare couch tonight" or "I cooked extra" and in most towns nobody has built that list. Spare rooms and spare dinners exist on nearly every street and the holders are unfindable because the holders never raised a hand anywhere a hungry person can see.

## Gap four: hands and authority

An AI model cannot carry a plate or unlock a door or pay a bill. An AI model holding a payment method that some person or agency has approved could order a hot meal to the car's location and book a motel room within minutes because those services already run all day for anyone holding money.

## What the four gaps add up to

Money and permission are the block because owners and landlords and lawmakers have chosen to release food and rooms mainly to people who can pay and the people who made that choice could choose differently tomorrow.

## What an AI chatbot with web search can do today

A person can name a town and an AI chatbot with web search can look up today's free meals and food pantries and shelter beds and overnight parking programs for people sleeping in cars and can list hours and entry conditions. The chatbot needs to list the risks too because some shelters turn people away when full and theft happens in shared rooms and some staff phone police about a person in visible distress.
