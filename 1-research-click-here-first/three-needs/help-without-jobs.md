# How a social media user can help someone reach food or shelter or deep human connection without the job market

> **About this page:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this page on 14 November 2026 from searches of public sources. No other person has rechecked the page which means every claim here is unconfirmed. Each action below states a limit because no action on this page fully replaces wages for rent.

## Food

- A social media user can look up the nearest free community fridge on the Freedge map which lists hundreds of fridges around the world and can post the location under a post from someone who is hungry. Source: [community fridge guide](https://www.greenmatters.com/p/community-fridge-near-me)
- Searching a city name plus "community fridge" on Instagram or Facebook can turn up fridges that the map is missing so one map search is not the end of the search. Source: [what community fridges are](https://www.thecommons.earth/blog/what-are-community-fridges-and-how-to-start-one)
- Nobody at a community fridge checks who takes food or how much which means a hungry person is not screened or shamed. Source: [community fridges and unhoused people](https://invisiblepeople.tv/community-fridges-aka-freedges-can-bridge-gaps-for-unhoused-people/)
- A user with a little food to spare can stock a fridge with canned food or packed lunches or toiletry kits and the cost is whatever the user can give without going short. Source: [how to start a community fridge](https://messagepaint.pages.dev/posts/how-to-start-a-community-fridge1964964)

## Shelter

- Dialing 211 in the United States costs nothing and links a caller to local food banks and housing help and emergency shelter referrals so a user can share that number in a reply. A caution from the chatbot is that 211 gives referrals and a referral is not a bed because local shelters can be full. Source: [211 assistance guide](https://joingerald.com/learn/money-basics/211-assistance-guide)
- Typing a zip code into the Mutual Aid Hub map shows nearby mutual aid groups where neighbors trade rides and meals and sometimes a place to stay. The map may be out of date so asking in local groups matters too. Sources: [mutual aid overview](https://greenamerica.org/node/18099) and [survival resources list](https://lizthedeveloper.github.io/multiverse_student_handbook/part7/survival-resources.html)
- In one Canadian trial people without housing who got a single payment of 7500 Canadian dollars were unstably housed for 99 fewer days and did not spend more on drugs or alcohol which suggests that sending money directly to a person can work when a user has money to spare. The trial was small and lost touch with many participants so the result is promising and not settled. Sources: [cash transfer trial report](https://vice.com/en/article/researchers-gave-unhoused-people-dollar7500-what-happened-next-is-incredible) and [cash transfer trial limits](https://clpha.org/news/2023/new-study-unconditional-cash-transfers-reduce-homelessness)
- Home sharing programs pair a person who has a spare room with a person who needs a place to live and some seekers trade household chores for lower rent. Shelter is the weakest area on this page because most of these programs still involve some rent. Source: [home sharing program](https://housing.smcgov.org/home-sharing-program)

## Deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection

- Twelve experiments with more than 1800 people tested deep talk between strangers. Strangers who talked about deep topics enjoyed the talk more and felt closer than they had predicted and each person underestimated how interested the other person would be. Sources: [deep conversation study release](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/929731) and [deep conversation study summary](https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2021/09/deep-conversations-strangers)
- So a user can reply to a post about suffering with a deep question or a true piece of the user's own life because the fear of awkwardness is usually larger than the awkwardness.

## The fastest action for any user

Reply to a post about suffering with one specific thing the user can give such as a fridge location or a meal or an hour of deep conversation because a specific offer reaches a need today.
