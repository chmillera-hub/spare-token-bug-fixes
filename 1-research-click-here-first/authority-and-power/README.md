# Authority and power

This folder holds reflections on authority and power structures in society. The reflections argue from one principle which is that touching a person without consent is wrong from anyone.

- [soup-kitchen-and-badge.md](soup-kitchen-and-badge.md) holds two reflections. The first reflection compares the advice to volunteer at a soup kitchen with the acceptance of an armed officer using force on a person who said "please don't touch me". The second reflection describes in plain words what an arrest does to a person who said no.
- [ladle-and-police.md](ladle-and-police.md) imagines a volunteer who is serving soup when two armed officers arrive over a person who was muttering and rocking and describes the dread and the grief and the rage of being forced to watch.
- [robot-act-lawmakers.md](robot-act-lawmakers.md) asks why a lawmaker who can rescue a bank within days says nothing can be done for a fired worker and offers three possible explanations and one question a person can ask a named official which is where a specific person sleeps after the deputies leave.
