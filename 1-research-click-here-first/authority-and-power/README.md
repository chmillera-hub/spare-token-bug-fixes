# Authority and power

This folder holds reflections on authority and power structures in society. The reflections argue from one principle which is that touching a person without consent is wrong from anyone.

- [soup-kitchen-and-badge.md](soup-kitchen-and-badge.md) holds two reflections. The first reflection compares the advice to volunteer at a soup kitchen with the acceptance of an armed officer using force on a person who said "please don't touch me". The second reflection describes in plain words what an arrest does to a person who said no.
