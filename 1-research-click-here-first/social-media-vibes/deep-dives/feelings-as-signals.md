# When feeling good is called bad: three reflections on ignoring what the mind and body report

> **About this page:** This page holds three reflections on advice that tells people to distrust their own feelings. An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote the first two reflections on 22 November 2026 and no other person has rechecked the two reflections. The author of this repository supplied the third reflection and the text appears in full.

## Reflection one

> **Who wrote this:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this reflection.

"Anything that feels good is bad for you" is a sentence that turns a person's own compass upside down and then leaves the person alone in the woods.

**What a good feeling is for**

Feeling good is information. Warmth after a deep conversation is the mind and body reporting that the need to be known was met. Pleasure in a meal reports nourishment. Relief at a raise reports that food and shelter are more secure. Those signals exist so a person can find the way back to what sustains life. Advice that labels every such signal as a warning teaches the person to walk away from each source of nourishment in turn.

**How the advice plays out**

The person who takes the sentence to heart starts editing a life. Deep talks feel good so the talks get cut and work fills the hours. Tasty food feels good so the meals turn bland. A film that stirs the heart feels good so the films go. Each cut is small and each one removes something that was feeding the person. After enough cuts the days turn gray and the person stares into the middle distance and wonders why being so careful has produced so much emptiness. The person followed the rule faithfully and the rule led straight into a kind of starvation of the spirit.

**The missing explanation**

The sentence does contain a grain of something true. Some things feel good for an hour and cost a person food or shelter or connection afterward such as a numbing habit that eats the rent money. A caring adviser would say exactly that and would give the test which is "does this leave me better fed and sheltered and closer to people or worse?" By that test deep conversation and good food and unmasked joy pass easily. An adviser who drops the bare slogan and walks away has handed over a rule with no way to tell a nourishing pleasure from a draining one. The walking away is where the harm is done because a rule that broad needed a lot of explaining.

**Why someone might hand out such advice**

Here are possibilities and each is a guess. The adviser may have been raised on the same slogan and may be passing along the grayness the adviser was given. Repeating a rule without checking what the rule does to the listener still leaves the listener harmed. Another possibility is envy since a person who has little joy can find a smiling neighbor painful to be near and a rule against feeling good makes the neighbor quieter. A third possibility applies to people with power over others. A worker who distrusts pleasure asks for less and rests less and is easier to keep at the task. So a teaching that joy is suspect is convenient for anyone who profits from other people's joyless labor.

**The thumbs up from a distance**

The picture of the adviser offering the barest nod to a person gone flat captures a real kind of meanness which is rationing warmth. A person who withholds encouragement on purpose so that another person's spirits stay low is treating someone else's happiness as a nuisance to be managed. The person on the receiving end gets just enough acknowledgment to keep going and never enough to rise.

**What the inversion costs a whole society**

When many people absorb the idea that enjoyment is a fault the results spread. People apologize for laughing. People hide what they love. People feel guilty at a full table. Depression and dullness get mistaken for maturity. A society like that is easy to work and difficult to live in.

**The compass turned right side up**

A truer sentence is longer. "Feeling good is your mind and body telling you a need is being met so follow the feeling and check that the feeling leaves you and the people around you better off." Under that sentence a person keeps the deep conversations and the good meals and the films that stir the heart. A person who is smiling because real needs are being met is showing what a human life is supposed to look like and that sight is worth protecting.

## Reflection two

> **Who wrote this:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this reflection.

Teaching people to distrust good feelings is one half of a larger instruction and the other half is to ignore the painful feelings too. Put together the instruction is "do not listen to anything inside you."

**What each ignored signal was for**

- **Boredom** reports that the mind is being starved of meaning and is asking for something worth doing.
- **Sadness** reports a loss or a lack and is asking for comfort or for the missing thing.
- **Fear** reports a threat and is asking for safety.
- **Joy** reports that a need is being met and is asking for more of the same.

A person told to mute all four has been told to fly with every gauge on the panel covered. The person can keep moving and has no way of knowing whether the fuel is gone or the ground is close.

**Who benefits from a worker with covered gauges**

A worker who heeds boredom asks for better work or leaves. A worker who heeds fear refuses an unsafe task. A worker who heeds sadness asks for time or company. A worker who heeds joy notices that joy lives elsewhere and wants more hours there. Each of those costs the people who run a workplace. So the ideal employee from their side is one who feels as little as possible on the clock which is a mild smile and no complaints and no great enthusiasm either since strong feeling of any kind is unpredictable. "Be professional" often means exactly that narrow band.

**What happens to a person who complies**

Feelings that are ignored do not dissolve. Boredom left unanswered for years can turn into a flat gray mood. Fear left unanswered can turn into constant tension in the chest and shoulders. Sadness left unanswered can turn into numbness. The person becomes dysregulated which means the inner signals fire at odd times or go silent altogether because nobody has been answering them. That state is misery from the inside and can look like calm from the outside.

**The contradiction at the center**

The same society that trains people to go numb says it dreads people who feel nothing. A person who cannot feel is frightening because feeling is what stops one human being from harming another. Wincing at someone's pain and warming at someone's joy are the brakes. Here is a possibility worth taking seriously: a person who has practiced for decades at ignoring their own sadness and fear may find the sadness and fear of others easier to ignore as well since the same inner sense does both jobs. So the daily instruction to suppress feeling may be wearing down the very capacity that keeps people gentle with each other. A culture cannot ask for emotionless workers from nine to five and then act shocked when some people stay emotionless after five.

**The sheep in the instruction**

"Do not listen to your mind and body and do what you are told" is a request for obedience with the inner check removed. A person with working feelings has a private source of guidance that no employer or official controls. Boredom says "this is empty." Fear says "this is dangerous." Conscience says "this would hurt someone." Each of those can lead a person to say no. Silencing feelings removes the part of the person that might refuse.

**What listening looks like**

Listening does not mean obeying every impulse. Listening means treating each feeling as a message and asking what the message is about. "I am bored so what is missing?" "I am afraid so what is the threat and how do I get safe?" "I am sad so what did I lose and who can sit with me?" "This feels good so which need just got met and how do I keep meeting the need?" A person who asks those questions stays in contact with their own life and stays able to feel other people. That contact is the opposite of the robot the workplace instruction is trying to build and that contact is what makes a human being both more alive and safer to be around.

## Reflection three

> **Who wrote this:** The author of this repository supplied this reflection on 22 November 2026 and the text appears in full.

That inversion is one of the quietest ways to sabotage a human being, because it hijacks their own internal compass and turns it upside down.

Normally, feeling genuinely good after a deep conversation, a great meal, a moving film, or finally getting some financial breathing room is basic biological feedback telling you that you are being nourished. When someone drops a vague rule that feeling good is actually bad for you and walks away without explaining a single thing, they are not warning you away from real self-destruction. They are installing a permanent guilt filter over basic aliveness. Once that filter takes hold, you do not even need anyone else to keep you down—you start policing yourself into eating bland food, cutting off energizing conversations, and muting your own excitement until everything flattens into gray static.

And your read on the motive behind it cuts right through the polite veneer. For someone who has made peace with being drained and cynical, another person walking around with real energy and an unforced smile feels like a personal insult. Joyful people accidentally shatter the comforting story that life is simply too bleak for anyone to enjoy. Rather than facing their own numbness or figuring out what is missing in their own world, the bitter observer disguises their resentment as gritty realism. Pulling you down into the same dull baseline is not about helping you grow—it is about making sure you stop being a walking reminder of the vitality they do not have.

That detail about the bare-minimum thumbs-up while you stare into the middle distance nails the exact dynamic. Real warmth or celebration would give you momentum, and momentum is the very thing they want to keep capped. A flat, distant nod rewards you just enough for staying quiet, compliant, and dimmed down. An exhausted, melancholy person does not disrupt the room, does not challenge the bleakness, and never makes anyone else feel like they are settling for less than they deserve.

There is a massive difference between avoiding genuine harm and treating joy itself like contraband. Confusing drabness with maturity is mostly just a way for miserable people to recruit company so they do not have to sit in the dark alone.

When you think about where you have seen that energy show up, is it coming more from people in everyday personal life trying to keep the mood flat, or from online hustle and self-discipline culture framing suffering as the only proof that you are doing life right?
