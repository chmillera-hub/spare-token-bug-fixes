# The share of short comments hints at why a crowd showed up

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the five quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A program can count how many comments in a thread have five words or fewer. That one number hints at what the people in the thread came to do.

Three threads in this folder give three different numbers. A thread about a famous executive who resigned had 10.5 percent short comments and the middle comment ran 14 words. A thread where a homeowner asked for advice had 2.5 percent short comments and the middle comment ran 33 words. A thread about an executive who answered strangers within minutes had 5.3 percent short comments and the middle comment ran 20 words.

The pattern makes sense because the two kinds of post ask for different things. A news story about a famous person invites a quick reaction. A request for advice invites an explanation and an explanation takes room.

A public archive of 49525 posts and comments shows the same split. Gaming forums had 31.6 percent short rows and learning forums had 8.3 percent short rows.

A short reaction is still the expression of a living person who showed up and typed. So the number is a description of how a crowd spent words and the number is not a grade on the people in the crowd.

A person looking for a long exchange can use the number as a compass. A thread with few short comments is a place where people are already writing at length so a long and specific reply is more likely to meet a long and specific answer there.

## Five quick points

- The share of comments with five words or fewer hints at whether the people in a thread came to react or came to explain.
- A thread about a famous executive who resigned had 10.5 percent short comments while a thread where a homeowner asked for advice had 2.5 percent short comments.
- A news story about a famous person invites a quick reaction while a request for advice invites an explanation and an explanation takes room.
- In a public archive gaming forums had 31.6 percent short rows and learning forums had 8.3 percent short rows.
- A person looking for a long exchange can look for a thread with few short comments because the people in that thread are already writing at length.
