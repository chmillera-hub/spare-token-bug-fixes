# A comment that a program could have written

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the five quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A comment such as "lol" or "wow" or "that sounds really hard to deal with" fits under almost any post. A comment that fits under any post says nothing about the post in front of the reader. A program could post such a comment as easily as a person could because the comment needs no understanding of what was written.

Two checks in this folder look for such comments in a public archive of 49525 posts and comments from one large site. One check counts words and found that about 1 row in 5 was five words or fewer. A short comment can still be specific and yet five words leave little room for a specific thought. The other check compares the longer rows with a list of 10000 interchangeable phrases of six words or more and found 2 word for word matches among 39658 longer rows. So a fixed list catches very few long hollow comments because people word a hollow thought in endless ways.

The cost of a hollow comment lands on the person who posted about something real. A person who writes three paragraphs about a hard week and receives "that sounds really hard to deal with" has been acknowledged and has not been answered. Enough replies of that kind can teach the person that detail is wasted effort so the person may stop sharing detail.

Pointing out hollow comments does not accuse anyone of being a program. A person can type "lol" with real warmth. The trouble is that a hollow comment gives the receiver no way to tell warmth from automation.

A specific reply fixes that trouble. A reply that repeats one detail from the post and asks one question about the detail could not have been written without reading the post. Such a reply proves attention and gives the conversation somewhere to go.

## Five quick points

- A comment such as "lol" or "that sounds really hard to deal with" fits under almost any post so the comment says nothing about the post the comment sits under.
- In a public archive of 49525 posts and comments about 1 row in 5 was five words or fewer which leaves little room for a specific thought.
- A list of 10000 hollow phrases of six words or more matched 2 of 39658 longer rows word for word because people word a hollow thought in endless ways.
- A hollow comment can come from a warm person and still gives the receiver no way to tell warmth from automation.
- A reply that repeats one detail from a post and asks one question about that detail proves that the replier read the post.
