# Counting what a comment asks for shows more than counting happy and sad words

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the five quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A common way to measure the mood of a thread is to count positive words and negative words. That count can mislead a reader because a cheerful sentence can ask for a harsh act.

Here is an example that the chatbot made up to show the problem. The comment "Good for you and I hope you send her packing" holds the warm words "good" and "hope". The same comment asks a homeowner to put a houseguest out of a home. A word count would score that comment as friendly while the houseguest could lose a place to sleep.

The work in this folder counts something different. Each comment gets a label for the behavior that the comment asks a person to carry out such as removing a person or paying a professional or adding care.

That count gave a clear picture of one thread of 364 comments. About 37 percent of the comments asked for a person to be controlled or removed or punished. About 10 percent of the comments asked for care to be added.

A count of requested behaviors is useful because a behavior is something that a reader can picture a person doing. A reader can picture a houseguest carrying bags out of a door. A reader has a harder time picturing a score of 0.4 on a mood scale.

## Five quick points

- A count of positive words and negative words can mislead a reader because a cheerful sentence can ask for a harsh act.
- The made-up comment "Good for you and I hope you send her packing" holds warm words and still asks a homeowner to put a houseguest out of a home.
- The work in the social media vibes folder labels each comment by the behavior that the comment asks for such as removing a person or adding care.
- In one thread of 364 comments about 37 percent asked for a person to be controlled or removed or punished and about 10 percent asked for care.
- A requested behavior is something a reader can picture such as a houseguest carrying bags out of a door so a behavior count teaches more than a mood score.
