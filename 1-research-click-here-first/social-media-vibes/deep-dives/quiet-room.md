# A quiet room is not proof of a safe room

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the five quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A smoke detector with the battery pulled out makes a room quiet. The quiet in that room comes from the missing battery and says nothing about whether wires are burning inside the wall.

Many groups of people treat emotional pain the way that room treats smoke. A family can agree that certain subjects stay off the table. A manager can tell workers to keep feelings out of the office. A group of friends can change the subject each time someone mentions feeling lonely. Each of those moves removes the alarm before the alarm can sound.

The people who remove the alarm often feel relief because an alarm is unpleasant and many people were not taught what to do when an alarm goes off. The cost lands on the person in pain. A person who learns that speaking about pain brings a cold shoulder or a lost job stops speaking. Losing a job can mean losing rent money and then losing housing so the lesson to stay quiet is backed by a real threat. The pain stays and the report of the pain disappears.

Then the people in charge look at the quiet and conclude that things are fine. The count of complaints fell so those people believe that suffering fell. Those people measured silence and called the silence peace.

A working alarm between two people can be small. One person asks "what is that pain asking for?" and then waits for the answer. That question puts the battery back in because the question tells the other person that a signal will be received and not punished.

## Five quick points

- A smoke detector with no battery makes a room quiet and the quiet says nothing about whether wires are burning in the wall.
- A family or a workplace that bans talk about feelings removes the alarm for emotional pain before the alarm can sound.
- A person who gets a cold shoulder or loses a job for speaking about pain learns to stay quiet because losing a job can mean losing housing.
- People in charge who see fewer complaints can wrongly conclude that suffering fell because those people measured silence and called the silence peace.
- Asking "what is that pain asking for?" and waiting for the answer puts the battery back because the question shows that a signal will be received and not punished.
