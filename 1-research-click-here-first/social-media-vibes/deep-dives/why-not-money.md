# Why money is not the number being raised here

> **About this page:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this reflection after the author of this repository asked why the work in this folder is not aimed at making money. Each point is the chatbot's interpretation of what the author has said and done and no person has rechecked the reflection.

## Why money is not the number the author is trying to raise

- The author's goal is measured in connection and money cannot buy that kind of connection. The author wants deep conversation and close human connection and a community of people living close by. A person who chooses to know another person cannot be purchased. The paid forms of connection are the forms the author criticizes because a paid helper is barred from connecting with a person outside the paid hour.
- A price would sit between a reader and the help. The author's writing argues against putting survival needs such as food and housing behind a paywall. Charging for writing that teaches people to care for their emotions would put emotional help behind a paywall too.
- Free things spread further. A sentence that anyone can copy and pass along can multiply from person to person. A sentence locked behind a payment stops at each person who cannot pay. So a price would lower the passing rate of the behaviors the author wants to spread.
- Selling changes what gets written. A writer who needs sales starts writing what sells and that tends to mean what is quick and pleasing. The author's writing asks readers to sit with suffering and to question their jobs and their schooling. Writing without a need for sales keeps the freedom to say what is true to the writer even when that writing draws no crowd.
- A paying audience creates debts. People who pay expect answers and service and a steady supply. The author has limited bandwidth and does not promise help that the author cannot deliver. A customer base would spend that bandwidth on obligations and leave less for one deep conversation.

## The numbers the author seems to watch in place of money

- Resonance: the author rates lines from 8 to 10 out of 10 by how hard a line lands.
- Usable comments: the author asks what share of the comments in a thread describe a behavior a reader can picture and copy such as 1 comment in 15 or 2 comments in 15.
- Depth of replies: the author sorts replies into those that engage with a person and those that are hollow phrases.
- Whether suffering goes down: the author's comments track whether boredom and loneliness and doubt feel heard.
- Spread: the author cares whether a behavior gets picked up and passed along by other people and by chatbots.
- The arrival of a person: underneath the other numbers the author seems to be watching for a human being who can meet the author at depth.

## Why a rising money number can feel like friction

Each hour spent on pricing and promotion and customer service is an hour not spent writing or talking with a person. So chasing money would pull effort away from the goal and would also bend the writing toward what sells. That is two costs with no gain toward connection.

## The author's view of money

The author sees the money system as prehistoric because food and shelter are locked behind payment. The author hopes for a new way of structuring society where a person's food and shelter no longer depend on money and hopes that change comes as quickly as possible.
