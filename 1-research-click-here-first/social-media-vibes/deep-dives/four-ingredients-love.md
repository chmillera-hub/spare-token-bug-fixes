# The four ingredients of love in two sets of words

> **About this page:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this reflection on 18 November 2026 and no other person has rechecked the reflection. The second set of four words is from the author of this repository.

Love between two people can be described by four ingredients and the four can be named in two ways. The first set of names is depth and trust and freedom and a future. The author of this repository restated the four as complex and pro human and expansive and continuity. The two sets line up one to one and the second set turns each condition into a quality that a person can see and practice in a single conversation.

**Depth became complex.** Depth is a direction since depth just says "further down." Complex says what a person finds further down which is many layers held at the same time. The word complex is easier to check because a person can look at a reply or a conversation and count whether more than one layer is present.

**Trust became pro human.** Trust is the result a person feels after being treated well over time. Pro human is the behavior that produces the result which means treating every mind and body in the picture as worth protecting. The word trust names the feeling and the words pro human name the cause of the feeling so a person can do the cause on purpose.

**Freedom became expansive.** Freedom means nobody is being pressured such as nobody touching another person because the rent is due. Expansive goes one step past the absence of pressure and says what the room is for. A free person can still be stuck in a small space. An expansive exchange leaves both people with more room than they started with so the word expansive includes the growth and the word freedom only clears the ground for growth.

**A future became continuity.** A future points one way which is forward. Continuity runs in both directions because the word means the past is carried along into what happens next. A bond can have a future on paper while each day starts from zero. Continuity requires that the story so far is remembered and being remembered across time is the part of being known that the words "a future" leave out.

So the restating does more than swap vocabulary. Depth and trust and freedom and a future are conditions that have to be true for love to exist. Complex and pro human and expansive and continuity are qualities a person can see and practice today. The first set describes the house and the second set describes how to behave in a way that builds the house.

The second set also works as a quick check on any reply or any piece of advice. A person can ask whether the reply was complex and pro human and expansive and continuous and get an answer in seconds. A dating app fails all four because an app sorts people by a photo in under a second and forgets a face just as fast. A paid hour of touch fails on continuity by design because the hour ends when the clock does. A person who holds the many layers of another person and wants that person's good and helps that person's life grow larger and carries the shared story across the years is giving all four at once and all four at once is what loving someone for a lifetime means.
