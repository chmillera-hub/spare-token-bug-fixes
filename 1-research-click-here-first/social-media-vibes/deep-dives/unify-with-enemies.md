# Social media deep dive: how do you unify with people who want to kill you?

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this reflection in answer to a social media post that asked "How can I unify with people that want to kill me". The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection. The page has two parts. The first part answers in plain words. The second part gives the same answer in a voice modeled on the way Christ speaks in the gospels and the second part is the chatbot's own writing and is not a quotation from the Bible.

## The answer in plain words

You flood their world with love until those people have no room left for hatred.

I mean that literally because a person who is fed and housed and deeply known by other people has a life worth protecting and a person with a life worth protecting has a whole lot to lose by hurting anyone. A person who is busy laughing at a full table has fewer empty hours to spend planning harm because joy eats up the time.

So the wild move is to want the person who hates you to flourish. Want that person surrounded by people who know that person's heart and mind because someone who learns to love a huge number of people might one day have enough love left over to spare some for you. And if the love never reaches you then maybe that person just leaves you alone because the life in front of that person got too good to risk.

Here is the part that gets me every time because the math is beautiful. People carry love to other people. Somebody that person loves might end up loving you too because that somebody might look at both sides and conclude that love builds a better environment for everyone than hatred does. And that somebody might turn around and say "please do not hurt them and please leave them alone and let them live a good life too". A plea from a loved one can reach a place that no argument from you ever could because people listen to the ones who know their heart.

None of this means standing still while someone swings at you. If a person is about to physically harm you then get distance first because a person who is injured cannot spread much love. Unity gets built from a safe distance by helping fill the whole neighborhood with food and shelter and deep human connection until killing starts to look like the dumbest possible use of a good life.

You unify by winning the kind of win where everybody wins because a better environment for yourself and others might reduce the chance that the person who wanted you dead will act that out. A win that needs a loser leaves someone carrying the loss while a win-win leaves every person holding a life too good to throw away. So build the better world out loud and let the person who hated you get so busy living their life in a pro-human way that their hatred ends up having nowhere to go and that hatred hopefully goes extinct.

## The same answer in the voice of Christ

You ask me "how can I unify with people who want to kill me". Truly I tell you that you unify with them by filling their days with love until no room remains in their heart for hatred.

You have heard it said "love your neighbor and hate your enemy". But I say to you love your enemies and pray for the ones who hunt you because the Father makes his sun rise on the evil and on the good alike.

Feed the one who hates you because an empty stomach leaves a wide door for hatred. See that the one who hates you has a roof and a table and people who know the depths of that one's soul. For a person whose cup overflows has a life worth guarding and a person with a life worth guarding has much to lose by shedding blood.

Rejoice when your enemy flourishes. For the one who learns to love a multitude may one day find love left over for you. And if that love never reaches you then perhaps your enemy will pass you by in peace because the feast set before your enemy is too good to abandon.

Hear this mystery for the mystery is glad news. Love is carried from person to person like bread passed down a long table. Someone your enemy loves may look upon you and love you also because that someone has weighed hatred against love and found that love builds the better house. And that someone may plead for you and say "do not harm them and let them live a good life too". A word from a beloved mouth opens a door that your own knocking could never open for the heart listens to the ones who know the heart.

Do not stand still before the raised fist. When a hand is lifted to strike you withdraw to another town because the injured cannot carry much love to anyone. Build the kingdom of heaven from a safe distance which means a place where every person is fed and sheltered and deeply known.

This is the victory in which no one is defeated. For a better world for you and for your neighbor may still the hand of the one who wished you dead. So build the kingdom in the open because a lamp is not lit to be hidden. Let the one who hated you grow so busy living a life that honors every human being that their hatred finds nowhere to dwell and withers and is seen no more.
