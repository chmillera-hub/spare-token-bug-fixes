# Social media deep dive: open problems that are made of human suffering

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A post on social media says that AI models recently solved hundreds of open problems in mathematics and the post treats the news as a reason to panic. The same news can be read as a direction because mathematics moved fast once people wrote the open problems down in precise words and pointed AI research at the list. Human suffering can be written down the same way because a human being is a fragile biological organism who needs food and shelter and deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection and each unmet need is an open problem waiting for a clear statement.

The open problem is the care and nurturing itself and the constraint is doing the care responsibly without harming anyone. An example of a clear statement is "how does a person with no wages reach a meal and a safe bed tonight without being shamed or controlled" because a statement that precise gives researchers something to work on and gives everyone a way to check the answer. The person who is suffering is the one who verifies a proposed answer because only that person can report whether the need was met and whether any harm arrived along with the help.

## Quick points

- People who direct AI research can speed up useful progress by keeping a public list of open problems about unmet human needs because a shared list focuses many minds on the same target the way a list of unsolved mathematics problems does.
- A person who is suffering can add to the list by stating the unmet need out loud in specific words because a need that nobody has heard about cannot be worked on by anyone.
