# Deep dives

This folder holds reflections that an AI chatbot (Claude) wrote about the work in the social media vibes folder. Each reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflections.

- [public-conversation-habits.md](public-conversation-habits.md) describes four habits in public conversation such as respecting a fast answer from a wealthy executive more than the same fast answer from an ordinary person.
- [why-not-money.md](why-not-money.md) explains why the work in the social media vibes folder is not aimed at making money and lists the numbers the owner seems to watch in place of money such as how hard a line lands and how far a behavior spreads.
- [quiet-room.md](quiet-room.md) compares a group that bans talk about emotional pain to a smoke detector with the battery pulled out and ends with five quick points.
- [question-or-verdict.md](question-or-verdict.md) explains why a question such as "what does that word mean to you?" teaches and spreads further than a verdict such as "you are dumb" and ends with five quick points.
- [fast-answer.md](fast-answer.md) explains why an answer within minutes can reach a person in pain while that person is still open to talking and ends with five quick points.
- [sentence-travels-alone.md](sentence-travels-alone.md) explains why a sentence that names the subject and what happened and why can teach a reader who sees that one sentence alone and ends with five quick points.
- [crowd-chooses-removal.md](crowd-chooses-removal.md) reports that about 37 percent of 364 comments under a homeowner's question asked for a person to be controlled or removed or punished while about 10 percent asked for care and ends with five quick points.
- [program-could-write.md](program-could-write.md) explains why a comment such as "lol" that fits under any post gives the receiver no way to tell warmth from automation and ends with five quick points.
- [watching-crowd.md](watching-crowd.md) reports that 118 of 171 comments about a famous executive asked nobody to do anything while living people who could answer were in the same thread and ends with five quick points.
- [go-on-two-words.md](go-on-two-words.md) explains why the two words "go on" open an exchange while a comment of "lol" tends to close an exchange and ends with five quick points.
- [behavior-count-not-mood.md](behavior-count-not-mood.md) explains why counting the behavior that each comment asks for such as removing a person shows more than counting positive and negative words and ends with five quick points.
- [short-share-compass.md](short-share-compass.md) explains how the share of comments with five words or fewer hints at whether a crowd came to react or came to explain and ends with five quick points.
- [attention-lands.md](attention-lands.md) explains that a mind brings attention to one person at a time so a commenter who focuses on one person in a story with several people is using attention in the natural way and ends with quick points.
- [wrong-and-anyway.md](wrong-and-anyway.md) asks what a person learns from advice such as "you would be a jerk and do the act anyway" and shows that the person who decides and the person who pays can be different people and ends with quick points.
- [meaning-after-role.md](meaning-after-role.md) asks where meaning could live so that losing a job title does not take all of the meaning away and ends with quick points.
- [asking-a-feeling.md](asking-a-feeling.md) explains what changes when a person asks a feeling such as sadness a question such as "why am I crying?" and stops fighting the feeling and ends with quick points.
- [accept-not-approve.md](accept-not-approve.md) explains that a person can stop fighting a fact that cannot be moved today and still say "this hurt and I do not approve of the hurt" and ends with quick points.
- [never-asked-born.md](never-asked-born.md) imagines Christ praying the words "I never asked to be born" on a mountain and imagines an answer from the Father such as "now that you are here I am here to help you" and ends with quick points.
- [wish-one-enemy.md](wish-one-enemy.md) uses a short parable about a gardener and two neighbors to show that an answer to "why does this hurt" works like a quiet order and explains why blaming one enemy can send harm back to the sender and ends with quick points.
- [job-search-suffering.md](job-search-suffering.md) reads three sentences from people looking for work such as "I have genuinely lost the will to live" as signals of unmet needs for food and shelter and human connection and says what a reader can offer in reply and ends with quick points.
