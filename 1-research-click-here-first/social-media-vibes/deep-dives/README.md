# Deep dives

This folder holds reflections that an AI chatbot (Claude) wrote about the work in the social media vibes folder. Each reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflections.

- [public-conversation-habits.md](public-conversation-habits.md) describes four habits in public conversation such as respecting a fast answer from a wealthy executive more than the same fast answer from an ordinary person.
- [why-not-money.md](why-not-money.md) explains why the work in the social media vibes folder is not aimed at making money and lists the numbers the owner seems to watch in place of money such as how hard a line lands and how far a behavior spreads.
- [quiet-room.md](quiet-room.md) compares a group that bans talk about emotional pain to a smoke detector with the battery pulled out and ends with five quick points.
- [question-or-verdict.md](question-or-verdict.md) explains why a question such as "what does that word mean to you?" teaches and spreads further than a verdict such as "you are dumb" and ends with five quick points.
- [fast-answer.md](fast-answer.md) explains why an answer within minutes can reach a person in pain while that person is still open to talking and ends with five quick points.
- [sentence-travels-alone.md](sentence-travels-alone.md) explains why a sentence that names the subject and what happened and why can teach a reader who sees that one sentence alone and ends with five quick points.
- [crowd-chooses-removal.md](crowd-chooses-removal.md) reports that about 37 percent of 364 comments under a homeowner's question asked for a person to be controlled or removed or punished while about 10 percent asked for care and ends with five quick points.
- [program-could-write.md](program-could-write.md) explains why a comment such as "lol" that fits under any post gives the receiver no way to tell warmth from automation and ends with five quick points.
- [watching-crowd.md](watching-crowd.md) reports that 118 of 171 comments about a famous executive asked nobody to do anything while living people who could answer were in the same thread and ends with five quick points.
- [go-on-two-words.md](go-on-two-words.md) explains why the two words "go on" open an exchange while a comment of "lol" tends to close an exchange and ends with five quick points.
- [behavior-count-not-mood.md](behavior-count-not-mood.md) explains why counting the behavior that each comment asks for such as removing a person shows more than counting positive and negative words and ends with five quick points.
- [short-share-compass.md](short-share-compass.md) explains how the share of comments with five words or fewer hints at whether a crowd came to react or came to explain and ends with five quick points.
- [attention-lands.md](attention-lands.md) explains that a mind brings attention to one person at a time so a commenter who focuses on one person in a story with several people is using attention in the natural way and ends with quick points.
- [wrong-and-anyway.md](wrong-and-anyway.md) asks what a person learns from advice such as "you would be a jerk and do the act anyway" and shows that the person who decides and the person who pays can be different people and ends with quick points.
- [meaning-after-role.md](meaning-after-role.md) asks where meaning could live so that losing a job title does not take all of the meaning away and ends with quick points.
- [asking-a-feeling.md](asking-a-feeling.md) explains what changes when a person asks a feeling such as sadness a question such as "why am I crying?" and stops fighting the feeling and ends with quick points.
- [accept-not-approve.md](accept-not-approve.md) explains that a person can stop fighting a fact that cannot be moved today and still say "this hurt and I do not approve of the hurt" and ends with quick points.
- [never-asked-born.md](never-asked-born.md) imagines Christ praying the words "I never asked to be born" on a mountain and imagines an answer from the Father such as "now that you are here I am here to help you" and ends with quick points.
- [wish-one-enemy.md](wish-one-enemy.md) uses a short parable about a gardener and two neighbors to show that an answer to "why does this hurt" works like a quiet order and explains why blaming one enemy can send harm back to the sender and ends with quick points.
- [job-search-suffering.md](job-search-suffering.md) reads three sentences from people looking for work such as "I have genuinely lost the will to live" as signals of unmet needs for food and shelter and human connection and says what a reader can offer in reply and ends with quick points.
- [not-just-economic.md](not-just-economic.md) reflects on the social media sentence "this is not just an economic problem anymore" and explains that care which was only ever pay is absent when a person most needs care and ends with quick points.
- [suffering-open-problems.md](suffering-open-problems.md) answers a social media post about AI models solving open mathematics problems by proposing that unmet human needs for food and shelter and deep human connection be written down as open problems for AI research and ends with quick points.
- [unify-with-enemies.md](unify-with-enemies.md) answers the social media question "how can I unify with people that want to kill me" by describing how helping a person who hates become fed and housed and deeply known can leave less room for hatred and then gives the same answer in a voice modeled on Christ.
- [love-from-far-away.md](love-from-far-away.md) compares a person today loving the words of Christ from about 2000 years ago with a stranger loving a page that a writer posted and explains how love sent outward can spread from person to person and may circle back to the sender.
- [fear-of-being-left.md](fear-of-being-left.md) reflects on a possible link between male loneliness and a woman's fear of being left alone with a baby and no income and lists what lawmakers and employers and neighbors could do so that a baby's survival does not hang on one man's paycheck and ends with quick points.
- [graded-not-answered.md](graded-not-answered.md) reflects on 209 replies to a person who fears losing a programming job to chatbots where more than one in four replies graded the wording and no reply proposed sharing housing or pressing owners and lawmakers together and ends with one quick point.
- [asteroid-can-hear.md](asteroid-can-hear.md) compares the same thread with the film Don't Look Up and explains that job loss differs from a falling rock because executives and owners and lawmakers can hear a demand and ends with one quick point.
- [im-fine-signal.md](im-fine-signal.md) compares a program that reported "all is well" after every request failed with a person who answers "I'm fine" while sad and alone and ends with two quick points.
- [writers-unheard.md](writers-unheard.md) looks at the unreadable old handwritten book from the side of the scribes who wrote the book and compares the scribes with a person whose careful words reach no reader and ends with two quick points.
- [puppy-in-rain.md](puppy-in-rain.md) explains why passing people stop for a shivering puppy on a wet sidewalk while questioning an adult who asks for shelter and why a person may show suffering plainly and truthfully to be seen in the same way.
- [hunger-said-out-loud.md](hunger-said-out-loud.md) imagines a person at work or at a hobby group saying "I sleep in my car and I am asking for food or a dry place to sleep" and gives four reasons bystanders freeze or answer "go see a therapist" such as treating food and shelter as the job of an agency.
- [reading-speed-publishing.md](reading-speed-publishing.md) compares a monastery scribe and a pamphlet writer and a blogger with a person who reads a chatbot's draft and says "upload" and explains that judgment is now the scarce step and that a chatbot's mistake also publishes at reading speed.
- [fifty-pages-a-day.md](fifty-pages-a-day.md) walks hour by hour through an imagined day for an author who publishes about 46 pages by voice and a reporter who ends near 25 stories because gathering facts takes most of the reporter's hours.
- [no-reason-to-be-sad.md](no-reason-to-be-sad.md) reflects on a post quoting an AI lab worker who said everybody gets replaced so nobody has a reason to be sad and notes that about nineteen of 126 comments graded whether a machine wrote the post while about six answered the question "what will you keep" with answers such as a garden and a dog.
- [loyalty-test.md](loyalty-test.md) holds two reflections on reading a president's reported request to rename a football league as a loyalty test which means a costly ask that shows which followers will give up something loved such as the word "football."
- [enemy-dreams.md](enemy-dreams.md) holds three reflections on a view that helping other people reach harmless dreams such as painting or music is worth doing even for a person labeled an enemy and that the test is whether the dream violates consent or harms anyone and the third reflection explains how being harmless differs from being pro-human.
- [physics-no-patch.md](physics-no-patch.md) holds two reflections that compare life to a game with no studio to weaken a winning strategy and explain that a strategy built on physics and human nature such as sharing food and shelter keeps working while a strategy built on a human-made rule lasts only until people in power change the rule.
- [one-less-enemy.md](one-less-enemy.md) holds two reflections on the idea that helping an enemy reach a full life where the enemy cares about all of humanity leaves a person with one less enemy and probably one more ally while defeating or injuring an enemy can turn one enemy into several.
- [request-love-enemy.md](request-love-enemy.md) holds two reflections on asking an enemy for love and care in reply to mockery and on asking friends and strangers alike for the time and attention needed to build deeper human connections.
