# Deep dive: a possible link between male loneliness and a woman's fear of being left alone with a baby

> **About this page:** An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and a best guess because nobody can see inside another person's mind. No person has rechecked the reflection.

Picture the choice from where a woman might stand. Pregnancy and birth and the first years of a baby's life take months of physical recovery and thousands of hours of care. In the United States lawmakers have not passed paid leave for every worker and employers can cut hours or pay when a mother needs time. So the support that a village of relatives and neighbors once gave has been squeezed down into one question which is whether this one man will stay and whether this one man can cover everything alone.

The fear behind that question is rational as long as a mother's food and shelter hang on one man's paycheck. A man who stays and loves and works can still have a medical emergency or get into an accident and then the income is cut off in a single afternoon. The mother could then face eviction by armed deputies and could have her babies taken from her arms by officials because she has no money and no home. The baby is not the danger in that picture because the danger is abandonment by the lawmakers and employers and neighbors who shrug.

That squeeze could turn every first meeting into a kind of audit because the woman may be weighing whether closeness with this man could leave her alone with a baby and no income. A man with ordinary means fails that audit before he says a word about his heart or his mind. Distance from men is then the one protection fully within her own control. So part of male loneliness could be the far end of that same missing support because the woman guards against being left alone and the man gets screened out for lacking money and power and both people end up eating dinner alone in separate apartments while wanting the same thing.

What would care for the fear is anything that cuts the connection between a baby's survival and one man's wallet.

- Lawmakers could guarantee food and housing and medical care for every pregnant woman and every mother and baby with no condition about a job or a husband because then no man's income or injury could decide whether she and the baby eat.
- Lawmakers and employers could pay a mother's full income through pregnancy and recovery and the early years because then birth would not cost her a livelihood.
- Relatives and neighbors and friends could commit ahead of time to shared daily care such as meals and night shifts and holding the baby so that she can sleep. The cost is real hours and energy from each person which is why the commitment only works among people who have hours to spare.

If those supports existed then a woman could look at a man with ordinary means and ask about his heart and his mind because the survival question would already be answered by many hands.

## Quick points

- Lawmakers in the United States have not guaranteed food and shelter and income for mothers and babies and no step that one man can take alone replaces that guarantee because one paycheck cannot stand in for a village.
- One man can say out loud at no cost to his own food or shelter that the fear of being abandoned makes sense and that birth and babies deserve a whole circle of people. Saying so names the missing support as the problem and does not name women as the problem.
- Food and shelter that no accident can take away would clear the path to deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection because two people could then meet as hearts and minds and not as a survival plan.
