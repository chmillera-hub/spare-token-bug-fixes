# A sentence that can travel alone

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the five quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

Many readers meet a sentence with nothing around the sentence. A person scrolling sees one line of a post. A screenshot captures one line. A friend quotes one line in a message. A sentence that depends on the rest of the page falls apart in each of those cases because the reader lacks the missing parts.

Compare two sentences. One sentence says "that approach failed for the reasons above." A reader who sees that sentence alone learns nothing because the approach and the reasons are somewhere else. Another sentence says "telling a lonely person to stay positive fails because the advice gives the person nothing to do about being alone." A reader who sees that sentence alone walks away with a complete thought.

A sentence travels well when the sentence holds who or what the sentence is about and what happened and why. A reader can then picture the scene and test the claim against the reader's own life.

Writing this way costs some repetition because the subject gets named again and again. The repetition is a fair price since a repeated subject costs a reader a second while a missing subject can cost a reader the whole meaning.

Sentences built this way also spread further. A person can copy one sentence into a comment and the sentence still teaches. So each self-contained sentence is a small package that can pass from reader to reader without the page the sentence came from.

## Five quick points

- Many readers see one line of a post through scrolling or a screenshot or a quote so a sentence often has to work with nothing around the sentence.
- The sentence "that approach failed for the reasons above" teaches a lone reader nothing because the approach and the reasons sit somewhere else.
- A sentence teaches a lone reader when the sentence names the subject and what happened and why such as "telling a lonely person to stay positive fails because the advice gives the person nothing to do".
- Naming the subject again and again costs a reader a second while a missing subject can cost a reader the whole meaning.
- A self-contained sentence can be copied into a comment and still teach so the sentence can pass from reader to reader without the original page.
