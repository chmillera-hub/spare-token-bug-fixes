# Where does a person's attention land in a story with several people?

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote this short reflection and the quick points under the reflection. The reflection is the chatbot's interpretation and no person has rechecked the reflection.

A news article reported that a famous technology executive resigned after reports about affairs. The people in the thread mostly wrote about the executive. No comment in the thread was about caring for the executive's wife or family or for the women named in the news reports.

A human mind brings attention to one person at a time because holding several people in focus at once is very hard. So a commenter who focuses on one person in a story with several people is using attention in the natural way. That focus can land on any person in the story and each choice is a fair choice.

The observation about the thread is about where many separate minds landed. Each commenter picked one person and most commenters picked the same person. Here is a possibility for why that happened. The article was written around the executive so the executive was the person with enough detail for a reader to picture.

That raises a question a reader can ask with curiosity. When my attention has room for one person who do I pick and what made me pick that person?

A commenter who happens to pick a person the article barely described can add something new to a thread. A comment about what a person in the wife's position might need such as privacy and people to talk with gives other readers a person to picture who was hard to picture before. A reader who has no attention to spare for that owes nobody an apology because attention is limited for every person.

## Quick points

- A human mind brings attention to one person at a time so a commenter who focuses on one person in a story with several people is using attention in the natural way.
- A person's focus can land on any person in a story and each choice is a fair choice because limited bandwidth is a fact of being human and no reason for shame.
- Most commenters in a thread about a famous executive picked the executive which could be because the article gave readers the most detail about the executive.
- A reader can ask with curiosity who the reader's attention picked and what made the reader pick that person.
- A commenter who happens to pick a person the article barely described such as the executive's wife gives other readers a person to picture who was hard to picture before.
