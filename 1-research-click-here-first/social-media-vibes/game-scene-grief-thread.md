> **About this page:** The author of this repository supplied a screen recording of an anonymized social media thread on 22 November 2026. An AI chatbot (Claude) reworded every comment so that no comment appears in its original wording and wrote the short count at the end. No other person has rechecked the wording or the count. Advertisements and picture-only replies are not listed.

## What the post said

The poster is a trans woman who had been enjoying a newly released video game. She wrote that each time a conventionally attractive woman appears on the screen she is filled with dysphoria and isolation because she believes she will never reach even an average level of feminine beauty. She wrote that people tell her she is pretty and that she does not believe them. She then described a scene in which a pregnant character asks the player to feel her belly and wrote that she broke down crying with her head in her hands because she felt inadequate and alienated and empty as if a part of her were missing. She wrote that she felt foolish for crying over a made-up pregnant woman. In later edits she wrote that she knows she needs therapy and is working toward therapy and she thanked the commenters and the moderators for their kindness.
