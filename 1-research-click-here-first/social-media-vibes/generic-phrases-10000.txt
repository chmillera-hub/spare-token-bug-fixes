10000 GENERIC SOCIAL MEDIA PHRASES THAT COULD COME FROM A BOT AS EASILY AS FROM A PERSON

What this file is: a sample list of interchangeable comment phrases. Each phrase signals a reaction such as laughter or agreement or sympathy and carries no information and no request that a reader could act on.

How to use the list: when a comment matches a phrase here the comment alone gives no evidence about who wrote the comment or why. A living person could have typed the phrase and an automated account could have posted the same phrase. The way to find out more is to reply and ask the writer what led to the comment.

How the list was made: an AI chatbot (Claude) wrote about 1400 base phrases by hand and a small program combined the base phrases with common openers and closers such as "lol" or "bro" or an emoji until the list reached 10000 different lines. The combined lines are patterns of the kind seen on social media and the lines were not copied from any real comment.

Limit: a phrase on this list is not proof of a bot. A short reaction can be a real person's small way of being present.
