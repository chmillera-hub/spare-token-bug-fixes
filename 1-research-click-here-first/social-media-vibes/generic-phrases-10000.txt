10000 GENERIC SOCIAL MEDIA PHRASES OF SIX WORDS OR MORE THAT COULD COME FROM A BOT AS EASILY AS FROM A PERSON

What this file is: a sample list of interchangeable comment phrases. Each phrase signals a reaction such as laughter or agreement or sympathy and carries no information and no request that a reader could act on. Each phrase fits under almost any post which means the phrase says nothing about the post the phrase sits under.

Why every phrase has six words or more: a comment of five words or fewer is already counted as too short to carry a specific thought. This list covers the longer hollow comments that a count of short comments would miss such as "thank you so much for sharing this" or "I'm so sorry you're going through this".

How to use the list: when a comment matches a phrase here the comment alone gives no evidence about who wrote the comment or why. A living person could have typed the phrase and an automated account could have posted the phrase. The way to find out more is to reply to the writer and ask what the writer meant.

How the list was made: an AI chatbot (Claude) wrote 320 base phrases by hand in eight groups and a small program combined the base phrases with common openers and closers such as "lol" or "bro" or "for real" until each group held 1250 phrases of six words or more.

Limit: a phrase on this list is not proof of a bot. A warm person can type any of these phrases and mean the phrase.
