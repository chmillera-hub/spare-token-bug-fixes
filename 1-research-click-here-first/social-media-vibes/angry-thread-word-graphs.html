Most common words in a thread about being angry with God

A person posted a video on a social media site about being angry with God. These graphs count words in the commenters' own wording as collected from the thread. Each bar shows how many comments hold a word or a phrase or an idea. Tap a bar and more detail opens under the bar.

Open the graphs of what each comment did

Graph 1: ideas that the comments mention

Each idea groups several related words. One comment can count on several bars.

Graph 2: the twenty most common single words

Very common small words such as "the" and "you" and "just" are left out because those words appear in nearly every comment.

Graph 3: common two-word phrases

Phrases made of small words alone such as "it is" are left out.

An AI chatbot (Claude) made the counts with a small program and no other person has rechecked the counts which means every count here is unconfirmed. Some words in the collected text were misread or cut off. The counts cover 528 pieces of comment text which is more than the 458 anonymized comments because the raw text includes fragments that were too broken to anonymize. Quoted scripture inside comments is counted too which raises the counts for words such as "God" and "wisdom".
