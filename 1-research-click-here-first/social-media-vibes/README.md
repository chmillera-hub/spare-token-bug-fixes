# Social media vibes

This folder holds counts of what behaviors the commenters in a social media thread are asking other people to carry out because a count of requested behaviors shows the mood of a thread more clearly than a count of positive and negative words.

- [Tappable bar graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/bar-graph.html) opens a live page where each bar shows how many commenters asked for a behavior and a tap on a bar opens an explanation under the bar and a link at the top of the page leads to the anonymized comments.
- [wifi-block-thread.md](wifi-block-thread.md) counts the behaviors that 364 comments under one person's social media thread asked for after a homeowner asked whether she should block an AI chatbot on her home wifi to stop a houseguest from using the chatbot.
- [comments-reworded.txt](comments-reworded.txt) holds all 364 comments and the homeowner's post and replies from the same thread in anonymized form with wording written by an AI chatbot that keeps the meaning and the energy of each comment.
- [comments-with-labels.txt](comments-with-labels.txt) holds the same anonymized comments with the counted behavior labels under each comment so that a reader can check every count on the findings page.

Second thread (a famous executive who lost his job):

- [Tappable bar graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/ceo-bar-graph.html) opens a live page where each bar shows how many of 171 comments asked for a behavior after a news article reported that a famous technology executive resigned.
- [ceo-findings.md](ceo-findings.md) counts the behaviors that 171 comments under one person's social media thread asked for and lists seven findings.
- [ceo-comments.txt](ceo-comments.txt) holds the 171 comments in anonymized form.
- [ceo-labels.txt](ceo-labels.txt) holds the same anonymized comments with the counted behavior labels under each comment.

Generic phrases (phrases that a program could post as easily as a person):

- [generic-phrases-10000.txt](generic-phrases-10000.txt) holds 10000 interchangeable comment phrases in nine groups such as laughter and agreement and sympathy.
- [generic-phrases-test.md](generic-phrases-test.md) reports a test of the phrase list against 49525 posts and comments from a public archive and lists five findings.

The repository owner's own comments compared with the two threads:

- [Tappable bar graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/owner-comments-bar-graph.html) opens a live page where each bar shows what kind of energy 60 randomly picked comments by the owner carry.
- [owner-comments-findings.md](owner-comments-findings.md) compares the owner's 9134 comments with the two threads and with the generic phrase list.
- [owner-comments-sample.txt](owner-comments-sample.txt) holds the 60 sampled comments as excerpts in the owner's own wording with the counted labels.
- [owner-comments-full](owner-comments-full) is a folder that holds 6020 of the owner's comments in full in 24 small files that the GitHub search box can look inside.
- [other-writing](owner-comments-full/other-writing) is a folder inside the owner-comments-full folder that holds 919 entries of other writing by the owner such as monologues and stories and posts and notes.
- [Words graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/owner-comments-full/words-graph.html) and [phrases graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/owner-comments-full/phrases-graph.html) open live pages inside the owner-comments-full folder where each bar shows how many times one of the 25 most common words or phrases appears across the 6020 comments.
- [Sentences graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/owner-comments-full/sentences-graph.html) opens a live page inside the owner-comments-full folder where each bar shows how many of the 6020 comments contain one of the 25 most repeated sentences word for word.
- [themes-findings.md](owner-comments-full/themes-findings.md) and [goal-findings.md](owner-comments-full/goal-findings.md) sit inside the owner-comments-full folder and report how themes shift across the 6020 comments and what goal the owner states in the comments.
- [mini-model-findings.md](owner-comments-full/mini-model-findings.md) sits inside the owner-comments-full folder and reports what a tiny text model learned after training only on the 6020 comments.
- [mini-model-outputs.md](owner-comments-full/mini-model-outputs.md) sits inside the owner-comments-full folder and holds example outputs and repaired outputs and four tests of the tiny text model.

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote the pages in this folder and no other person has rechecked the counts which means every count here is unconfirmed.
