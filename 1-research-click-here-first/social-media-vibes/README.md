# Social media vibes

This folder holds counts of what behaviors the commenters in a social media thread are asking other people to carry out because a count of requested behaviors shows the mood of a thread more clearly than a count of positive and negative words.

- [Tappable bar graph](https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes/bar-graph.html) opens a live page where each bar shows how many commenters asked for a behavior and a tap on a bar opens an explanation under the bar and a link at the top of the page leads to the reworded comments.
- [wifi-block-thread.md](wifi-block-thread.md) counts the behaviors that 349 commenters in one Reddit thread asked for after a homeowner asked whether she should block an AI chatbot on her home wifi to stop a houseguest from using the chatbot.
- [comments-reworded.txt](comments-reworded.txt) holds all 364 comments and the homeowner's post and replies from the same thread with every comment rewritten in new words by an AI chatbot so that the meaning and the energy stay while a search engine cannot trace a comment back to the account that wrote the original.

An AI chatbot (Claude) wrote the pages in this folder and no other person has rechecked the counts which means every count here is unconfirmed.
