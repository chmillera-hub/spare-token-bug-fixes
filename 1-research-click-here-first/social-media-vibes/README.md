# Social media vibes

This folder holds written reflections on subjects such as loneliness and empty advice and loving an enemy and the folder also holds counts of what behaviors the commenters in a social media thread are asking other people to carry out because a count of requested behaviors shows the mood of a thread more clearly than a count of positive and negative words.

- [The "I'm fine" signal: when the outside report says all is well and the inside report says every door closed](deep-dives/im-fine-signal.md) compares a program that reported "all is well" after every request failed with a person who answers "I'm fine" while sad and alone and ends with two quick points.

- [A puppy in the rain: showing suffering plainly as a way to ask for shelter and food](deep-dives/puppy-in-rain.md) explains why passing people stop for a shivering puppy on a wet sidewalk while questioning an adult who asks for shelter and why a person may show suffering plainly and truthfully to be seen in the same way.
