What 364 comments asked a homeowner to do

A homeowner asked the readers of a social media site whether she should block an AI chatbot on her home wifi because a friend who is staying with her during a serious mental health crisis talks to the chatbot all day. Each bar below shows how many of the 364 comments asked for that behavior. Tap a bar and an explanation opens under the bar.
