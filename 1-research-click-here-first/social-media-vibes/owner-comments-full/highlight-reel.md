# Highlight reel from the mini models

This page collects lines from the mini models that the owner of this repository rated 8 to 10 out of 10 for resonance which means the line landed hard for the owner when the owner read the line.

A tiny text model invented every line on this page after learning only from the owner's writing. The model only imitates which words tend to follow which words and does not understand the writing or hold any belief. An AI chatbot (Claude) repaired the connecting words and capital letters and punctuation in each line so that the line reads as a grammatical sentence. The owner then chose the lines and trimmed some lines to the part that resonated. None of the lines is a real verse or a real quote.

The raw outputs behind these lines are on the pages named [second-model-outputs.md](second-model-outputs.md) and [mini-model-outputs.md](mini-model-outputs.md) in this folder.

## What the chosen lines share

An AI chatbot (Claude) compared the chosen lines and the notes below are the chatbot's reading and not the owner's words.

- Five of the nine lines open with "if" or "when" and then land on a conclusion which gives each line a starting point and a destination inside one sentence.
- Seven of the nine lines contain suffering or a suffering emotion and in each of those lines the suffering leads toward understanding or care or love.
- Three of the lines say what a being is such as "a being with a brain that is a part of a complex soul".
