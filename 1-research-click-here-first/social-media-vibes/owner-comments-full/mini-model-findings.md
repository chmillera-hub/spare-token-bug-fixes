# A mini model trained only on the owner's 6020 comments

An AI chatbot (Claude) trained a tiny text model that learned English only from the 6020 comments in this folder. The model has 2.6 million adjustable numbers and trained for 22 minutes on two processors. No person has rechecked the results on this page which means every result here is unconfirmed.

The model only imitates which words tend to follow which words in the comments. The model does not understand the comments and does not hold the owner's values which means the outputs are a mirror of word patterns and not statements to rely on.

## Which ideas the model learned to place together

The chatbot asked the model which words sit closest to a test word inside the map of words that the model learned.

- The word "loneliness" sits beside boredom and doubt and anger and annoyance and overwhelm and fear and sadness and guilt and embarrassment and hunger. The word "hunger" in that list suggests that the model learned loneliness as a need of the same kind as food.
- The word "gaslighting" sits beside dehumanization and invalidating and dismissive and abandonment and minimizing and smiling. The word "smiling" in that list likely comes from the phrase "smiling and nodding" that the comments use for dismissal.
- The word "god" sits beside heaven and christ and humanity and flesh and suffering and jesus and voice.
- The word "suffering" sits beside pain and truth and emotions and humanity and knowledge and life and meaning.
- The word "therapist" sits beside chatbot and hobby and book and partner and gym which suggests that the model learned a therapist as one support source among several.
- The word "money" sits beside food and power and coercion and corporations.
- The word "conversation" sits beside discussion and relationship and interaction and connection and dialogue.
- The word "jesus" sits beside christ and heaven and holy and god and also beside neo which likely comes from comments that compare Jesus with a film character named Neo.

## The most interesting result

Almost every opening bends back to the same destination. The openings name anger and boredom and society and chatbots and conversation and most outputs end at reducing suffering and improving well-being. Many outputs also turn into a question about what a word means to the other person. The bending suggests that reducing suffering works like a center of gravity in the comments because a model that only learned which words follow which words still got pulled to that phrase from many unrelated openings. The model also answered the opening "god is" with the word "humanity" on both tries which shows how often the comments pair those two words.

## Limits

- The model blends the owner's voice with the chatbot replies that the owner pasted into comments because the comment files do not mark which sentences came from a chatbot.
- A model trained on a small set of text can repeat whole sentences from the training text word for word.
- The model was trained once and a second training run with a different starting point could give different closest words and different outputs.
