# Example outputs and tests from the mini model

An AI chatbot (Claude) ran more tests on the tiny text model that was trained only on the 6020 comments in this folder. The page named mini-model-findings.md in this folder explains how the model was trained. No person has rechecked the results on this page which means every result here is unconfirmed.

The model only imitates which words tend to follow which words in the comments. The model does not understand the comments and does not hold any belief which means the outputs are a mirror of word patterns and not statements to rely on.

## Four tests

### Test 1: openings that have nothing to do with the themes of the comments

The chatbot gave the model 15 neutral openings such as "the weather today is" and "my favorite food is" and "the car was" and collected 75 outputs.

- 48 of the 75 outputs mention emotions which is 64 percent.
- 26 of the 75 outputs mention suffering which is 35 percent.
- 15 of the 75 outputs mention well-being which is 20 percent.

The chatbot then gave the model 15 openings on the themes of the comments such as "anger is" and "society is" and collected 75 more outputs. 41 of those 75 outputs mention emotions and 35 mention suffering and 20 mention well-being. The near-equal shares for emotions suggest that the model drifts to emotions and suffering even from an opening about weather.

Only 1 of the 150 outputs contains the question about what a word means to the other person. The findings page says that many outputs turn into that question and the larger test does not support that sentence because the sentence rested on 20 outputs.

### Test 2: a careful setting against a wild setting

The model has a dial that controls how safe or how surprising each next word is.

- On the careful setting the model wrote: loneliness is a signal that is a signal that is a signal to me that is a signal to me that i am not a signal to reflect on my emotional needs and then i can use to help me reduce my suffering and improve my well-being and peace.
- On the wild setting the model scatters into loose word strings that drop the themes of the comments.

The loop on the careful setting suggests that "a signal" is the most expected continuation after the word loneliness in the comments.

### Test 3: does the model copy sentences word for word

The chatbot checked each of the 150 outputs for the longest run of words that also appears word for word in the comments.

- The typical longest copied run is 6 words.
- The longest copied run in any output is 11 words and reads "to reduce my suffering and increase my well-being and peace and".
- 13 percent of the outputs hold a copied run of 8 or more words and no output holds a copied run of 12 or more words.

The short runs suggest that the model recombines phrases from the comments into new strings and did not copy a whole sentence in these 150 outputs.

### Test 4: more closest words inside the map of words that the model learned

- The word "lonely" sits beside disconnected and abandoned and safe and scared and vulnerable.
- The word "meaningless" sits beside dehumanizing and vague and useless and shallow and dismissive and gaslighting.
- The word "silence" sits beside control and hide and dismiss and ignore and ghost.
- The word "capitalism" sits beside supremacy and starvation and homelessness and corporations and authority.
- The word "death" sits beside awakening and starvation and abandonment and labor and control and isolation.
- The word "job" sits beside hobby and phone and advice and book and therapist.
- The word "love" sits beside value and respect and trust and hope and care and also beside fear and shame.
- The word "power" sits beside money and dominance and dopamine.
