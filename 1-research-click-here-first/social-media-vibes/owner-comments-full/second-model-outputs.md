# Outputs from a second mini model

An AI chatbot (Claude) trained a second tiny text model of the same size as the first mini model. The first mini model learned only from the 6020 comments in this folder. The second mini model learned from the comments plus the owner's other writing such as monologues and stories and posts and notes. No person has rechecked the results on this page which means every result here is unconfirmed.

Both models only imitate which words tend to follow which words. Neither model understands the writing or holds any belief which means the outputs are a mirror of word patterns and not statements to rely on. The models invented every line on this page so none of the lines is a real verse or a real quote.

On this page "first model" means the model trained on the comments and "second model" means the model trained on the comments plus the other writing.

### What a count of 40 outputs from each model shows

The chatbot generated 40 outputs for the opening "jesus said" from each model and counted what the outputs contain.

- Words such as God and Lord and Father and heaven appear in 21 of the 40 outputs from the first model and in 11 of the 40 outputs from the second model which suggests that the second model sounds less like scripture.
- Quotation marks appear in 30 outputs from the first model and in 15 outputs from the second model which suggests that the second model continues the opening as a scene more often than as a quoted verse.
- Emotion words appear in 10 outputs from the first model and in 14 outputs from the second model.
- Scene words such as "looks" and "eyes" and "says" appear in 9 outputs from the first model and in 12 outputs from the second model.
- The six outputs above from the first model hold broken non-words such as "citiz" and "coalots" and "woever" while the six outputs above from the second model hold almost no broken non-words.

- About 11 of the 42 answers from the second model open by asking something back such as "what does the facade mean to you?" while the first model did that in 1 of 24 answers.
- 7 of the 42 answers from the second model begin with the word "because" which is 17 percent while half of the answers from the first model began with the word "because".
- The answer about loneliness has boredom speaking twice which suggests that the second model learned emotions as speaking characters from the monologues.
- The second model attempted the question about dying while the first model skipped death in both tries.
- 17 of the 42 answers mention suffering and 18 mention emotions which shows that the same center holds in the second model.
