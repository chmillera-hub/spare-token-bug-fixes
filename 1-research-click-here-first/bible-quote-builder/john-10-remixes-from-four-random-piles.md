# Three remixes of John chapter 10 made by dealing the chapter into four random piles

This page holds three remixes of John chapter 10 from the World English Bible and each remix was made by dealing the 201 key phrases of the chapter at random into four piles the way a deck of cards is dealt and then building one paragraph from every phrase in each pile.
A key phrase is a short piece of about four words such as "i am the good shepherd" and the three remixes differ only in the random order of the deal.
An AI model (Claude) wrote the paragraphs on 11 November 2026 under rules that Charles set which allow free order and added joining words and a short list of the most used words of the chapter and changed pronouns and added punctuation and generic words in place of rarely used names.
Added or changed words carry stars and a program check confirmed that every dealt phrase appears in its paragraph except the two place names that were replaced ("beyond the jordan" became "across the river" and "in solomons porch" became "in a covered walkway").
The grades use seven sentence rules from the page named writing-rules-for-clear-chatbot-replies in the folder named writing-rules with a top score of 14 and the AI model graded paragraphs that the AI model wrote which means the grades are a rough guide.

Warning about changed wording: these paragraphs are not exact Bible quotes because about one word in three was added by the AI model and pronouns and names were changed which means a reader who wants the exact wording needs to open John chapter 10 in the World English Bible itself.

## What the three remixes showed

- All twelve paragraphs came out as small versions of the whole chapter (the shepherd and the door and then the argument about works) because John 10 repeats its main ideas so often that a random quarter of the phrases still holds a piece of every idea.
- The grades stayed within one point of each other (12 or 13 of 14 in all twelve paragraphs) which means the random order of the deal made little difference to how readable a pile could become.
- Between 28 and 39 percent of the words in each paragraph were added because random dealing leaves most phrases without their true neighbor (a neighbor lands in the same pile only about one time in four).
- The three remixes differ mostly in which details each pile happens to hold (for example which pile gets the wolf or the winter or the line about John) while the main ideas appear in every pile of every remix.
- A strong caution applies: the AI model knows John 10 and very likely leaned on that knowledge when choosing the added words which means the paragraphs show how well a reader who knows the chapter can rebuild the chapter and do not show what a reader with no knowledge of the chapter could do.
- Several paragraphs report that people picked up stones and each paragraph keeps the dispute as a question and an answer which means no paragraph presents harm to any person as good.
