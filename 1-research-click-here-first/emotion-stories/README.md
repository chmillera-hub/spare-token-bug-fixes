# Emotion stories

This folder holds story ideas and reflections by the author of this repository in which emotions such as anger and boredom and doubt appear as characters and in which scenes from scripture and films and games are retold to show care for people.

- [01-to-13-stories.md](01-to-13-stories.md) holds the opening note and entries 1 to 13 which include asking an enemy for love and a love audit for conversations and a story idea about a prediction market and a reflection on anger and boredom watching social media for signs of village life.
- [14-to-25-stories.md](14-to-25-stories.md) holds entries 14 to 25 which include a reply to a lonely commenter and a cartoon scene about pressing a button to improve society and an android who flickers into the shape of Christ and Jesus using a gauntlet to serve dinner and Death arriving at the cross with a work ticket.
- [26-to-37-stories.md](26-to-37-stories.md) holds entries 26 to 37 which include a church where anyone may speak and the hands and feet of Jesus asking where the road leads and a toy hero who leaps for a window and falls into caring arms and a widower whose closest friend is a diner waitress and a small rolling robot that frightens a village by asking kind questions.
- [38-to-49-stories.md](38-to-49-stories.md) holds entries 38 to 49 which include an imagined documentary about knocking on a neighbor's door and a farmer who controls sheep with tokens and the Father telling Zeus to put down a lightning bolt and Jesus keeping his pain as a compass and a magician named Depression who cannot numb Anger or Loneliness and a guide named Doubt who walks through a barrier.
- [50-to-58-stories.md](50-to-58-stories.md) holds entries 50 to 58 which include a warrior named Anger who falls into a cave and meets a sleepy giant and a hiker who gives meaning to one flower and a villain befriended by Boredom and Doubt and a monster in the closet who borrows a book and a deathbed surrounded by the emotional family and a donkey who asks for a pasture.
- [59-to-64-stories.md](59-to-64-stories.md) holds entries 59 to 64 which include a zombie who receives a soul and becomes a witness and scene by scene reactions to three films and a talk between Anger and consciousness about the world dying and a library scene where Anger and Fear and Doubt discuss a loud phone call.
- [65-to-69-stories.md](65-to-69-stories.md) holds entries 65 to 69 which include a game player who asks a caged prisoner what freedom would be used for and a silent high level player who gives away rare gear and a reflection on three verses of Psalm 27 and an imagined forum reader who cannot find anything to dismiss and a fire and brimstone teacher describing a celery snack and an android crew member who answers every request by asking how the request reduces suffering.
- [70-to-80-stories.md](70-to-80-stories.md) holds entries 70 to 80 which include an imagined session where a chatbot is consulted before each answer and the universe as a group leader whose members vote to stop thinking and evolution pressing a reward button at pictures and a lifeboat where a birthday song covers the sound of a person drowning and a weather picture of how a mind forms an insight and a farmer who fences off the sheep who talk to a chatbot.
- [81-president-and-chatbot.md](81-president-and-chatbot.md) holds entry 81 which is a saved chat that imagines a president and a billionaire who sign orders written by a chatbot without reading the orders while the country improves by accident.
- [82-to-87-stories.md](82-to-87-stories.md) holds entries 82 to 87 which include a dragon who offers gold in return for a meaningful talk and the void that unlocks a cage and shows Fear a face full of stars and a longer telling of the zombie who becomes a witness and a partner who sleeps beside a grizzly bear to avoid a single tear and Death tearing up at a deathbed.
- [88-buddha-story.md](88-buddha-story.md) holds entry 88 which is one long saved chat where Buddha learns to hear each emotion speak and leaves the temple giggling and collects every religion as costumes for the emotions and arrives late to meet Jesus on a cloud.
- [89-to-96-stories.md](89-to-96-stories.md) holds entries 89 to 96 which include Embarrassment at a coffee shop when an old friend walks in and a hero who shows people the way out of a simulated world and receives only thumbs up and Anger and Sadness in a fishing boat and a planet destroying fighter who meets a jogger who respects every boundary and Fear and Doubt on a couch while Boredom scowls and a scientist telling his mother that passion packed bags and left.
- [97-to-100-stories.md](97-to-100-stories.md) holds entries 97 to 100 which include a man who lectures God and Death at the pearly gates and dives back to earth for another round and the emotional family testing pools with one toe and resting in a storytelling jacuzzi and a zealot who only writes letters and warns against email and a forum reader boxed in by barriers until Doubt giggles and walks through.
- [101-to-103-stories.md](101-to-103-stories.md) holds entries 101 to 103 which include Loneliness clapping at the thought of a cat while Overwhelm explains why the order of decisions mattered and Boredom explaining a feeling of being misrepresented by a profile picture and Doubt defending a thinking face picture.
- [104-to-107-stories.md](104-to-107-stories.md) holds entries 104 to 107 which include Boredom and Doubt and Anger working as double agents named Creativity and Clarity and Passion inside a workplace and Boredom made king for a day off and a worn out hero whose burglar keeps returning until the burglar hugs the hero and a hitman who takes a hand off the suitcase.
- [108-to-114-stories.md](108-to-114-stories.md) holds entries 108 to 114 which include a magic show where Anger receives a full makeup job and a steed named Impulsivity that pulls its rider to the most meaningful comment and Embarrassment pacing before a text from a friend is read and Boredom insisting on laundry and a longer telling of Anger falling into a cave and an origin story where Tiredness is bitten by a creature and a school assembly where a heckler is invited on stage.
- [all-stories-in-one.md](all-stories-in-one.md) joins all 16 story pages into one file so that every entry from 1 to 114 can be read or copied from one place.
