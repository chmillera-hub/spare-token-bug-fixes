# Record of the chat behind the research pages

This page is a time-ordered record of one long chat between Charles and an AI chatbot (Claude) on 11 November 2026 which produced the research pages in this folder.
Each entry gives the request from Charles in his own words and then a short summary of what the chatbot did because the full replies of the chatbot run to many tens of thousands of words and would bury the requests.
Charles dictates by voice which means his requests read like speech and some words came out misspelled and the misspellings are kept as typed.
All times are United States Central time.

## The full chat word for word

Charles shared the whole chat as a public link and the link shows every word from both Charles and the chatbot up to the moment Charles made the link: https://claude.ai/share/a29d81cb-280d-4d44-a449-e84b9516b4f5
Charles refreshed the shared link on the evening of 11 November 2026 which means the same address now also shows the later part of the chat where the Bible quote builder rules and outputs were made and the pages named rules and best-outputs in this folder hold the results of that later part.
