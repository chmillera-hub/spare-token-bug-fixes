# Research pages and free bug fixes from Charles and an AI chatbot

These pages can also be read as a website at https://chmillera-hub.github.io/spare-token-bug-fixes/ and the pages and the bug fixes were made with the AI chatbot Claude Fable 5.2. The pages cover emotions and nonviolence and meeting human needs for food and shelter and deep human connection.

Click the folder named 1-research-click-here-first before anything else because that folder holds the pages written for people to read and the other folder holds only program files.

## 1-research-click-here-first

The folder named [1-research-click-here-first](1-research-click-here-first) holds short plain-language pages and documents that a visitor can read in a few minutes each.

Each link in the first list opens a whole folder and the folder page lists every page inside the folder with one sentence that says what the page holds.

- [Social media vibes](1-research-click-here-first/social-media-vibes) holds 51 written reflections on subjects such as loneliness and empty advice and loving an enemy plus counts of what behaviors the commenters in social media threads ask other people to carry out.
- [Three needs](1-research-click-here-first/three-needs) holds research about meeting three basic human needs without force (food and shelter and deep meaningful emotionally and spiritually resonant human connection) which means a page of actions a social media user can take and a page of tested nonviolent replacements for six uses of force such as armed police and eviction.
- [Emotion stories](1-research-click-here-first/emotion-stories) holds story ideas and reflections by the author of this repository in which emotions such as anger and boredom appear as characters.
- [Spiritual reflections](1-research-click-here-first/spiritual-reflections) holds scripture passages collected by the author of this repository in the wording of the World English Bible together with the author's reflections on the passages and two stories about Jesus (a prayer spoken in a garden while soldiers watch and a return to Times Square) and a text message exchange in which the author asks church service workers for small groups.
- [The Emotional Framework](1-research-click-here-first/emotional-framework) holds a document that the author of this repository wrote which treats each emotion as a member of an inner family and lists 19 emotions with the purpose and need of each one so that a person or an AI agent can look up what an emotion is signaling.
- [Authority and power](1-research-click-here-first/authority-and-power) holds reflections on authority and power structures in society which argue from the principle that touching a person without consent is wrong from anyone.
- [Kindness games](1-research-click-here-first/kindness-games) holds small computer tournaments about the instruction "love your enemies" which test whether a player who always makes kind moves can do well when the player may leave a harmful partner or try a low-stakes sample game or see a move in advance or check a visible record.
- [Animated videos](1-research-click-here-first/animated-videos) holds two animated videos with voices and music and subtitles (a story about a person who stomps clearance food for angry clicks and a teaching about feeding an enemy) and plain text files of every spoken line.
- [Christ voice scripts](1-research-click-here-first/christ-voice) holds short scripts where Christ talks with the warrior prince Vegeta (four scripts in word patterns counted from the red-letter words of the World English Bible and three interactions in a two-part shape counted from passages rated as hitting hard).
- [Book of Mormon readings](1-research-click-here-first/book-of-mormon-readings) holds chapters of the Book of Mormon in the original wording and in modern English starting with chapter 3 of the Book of Mosiah.
- [Bible quote builder](1-research-click-here-first/bible-quote-builder) holds an experiment that remixes short phrases from the World English Bible into replies to comments and headlines and the folder has its own list that starts with the finished outputs and the rules.
- [Writing rules for clear chatbot replies](1-research-click-here-first/writing-rules) holds a set of writing rules for clear chatbot replies with before and after examples and a plain text copy of the same rules.
- [Unanswered questions](1-research-click-here-first/unanswered-questions) holds experiments where the chatbot hunted for public questions that nobody had answered and then tried to answer the questions (a mistake found in a published number list and a partial answer about an old Turkish coin and sixty-four more).
- [The Mobius strip picture of physics](1-research-click-here-first/mobius-strip-picture-of-physics) holds a document that pictures strange behaviors in physics with twisted paper ribbons and uses no equations.
- [Word evolution](1-research-click-here-first/word-evolution) holds an experiment where text is copied with small mistakes over and over while a reader picks which copies survive and the folder includes a live program with sliders.
- [Voynich manuscript](1-research-click-here-first/voynich-manuscript) holds three pages about an unreadable book from the early 1400s which cover who probably made the book and where the book was probably made and a second run of the spelling-pattern tests.
- [Squad Rush game](1-research-click-here-first/squad-rush-game) holds a free phone game that runs in a web browser where a squad of blue soldiers shoots up a road through ten stages of gates and cages and a boss and the folder gives the address for playing the game.
- [Zeta sums](1-research-click-here-first/zeta-sums) holds computer search results about the numbers zeta(3) and zeta(5) and zeta(7) and zeta(9) which include two formulas that write zeta(3) purely as sums over powers of the golden ratio and a formula for zeta(5) with no extra pieces in a base near 0.7549 and four long formulas with every whole-number coefficient written out.

Each link in the second list opens a single page because the page is a whole project with no other pages beside the page.

- [Hiding a book by splitting pages](1-research-click-here-first/hiding-a-book.md) shows how much sense is left when a 1000-page biology book is dealt into four piles in four different ways.
- [Scattered pages reflection](1-research-click-here-first/scattered-pages-reflection.md) is a short reflection that compares a life told in separate parts to separate listeners with a book dealt into piles and asks who has been handed enough neighboring pages to follow the story.
- [Human needs as research topics](1-research-click-here-first/human-needs-as-research-topics.md) is a thought experiment that answers the question of what AI systems should work on next by pointing at living people whose needs are not being met such as a person who is lonely or a person in unexplained pain.
- [Record of the chat behind the research](1-research-click-here-first/chat-record.md) lists every request the repository author made in the chat that produced the research pages in his own words with a short note on what the chatbot did and gives the public link to the full word-for-word chat.
- [Free bug fixes video](1-research-click-here-first/free-bug-fixes-video.md) points to a short video of 371 free bug fixes for public software projects.

The public link to the full chat behind the research is https://claude.ai/share/a29d81cb-280d-4d44-a449-e84b9516b4f5
