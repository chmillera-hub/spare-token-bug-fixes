# Program files behind the research pages

This folder holds only program files and a researcher can rerun the tests with these files while a casual visitor can skip this folder.

- The folder voynich-scripts holds four Python files that compute the "next page" scores for the Voynich manuscript and the scripts expect the typed copies named ZL3b-n.txt and IT2a-n.txt and RF1b-e.txt from the site voynich.nu inside a folder named data.
- The folder voynich-scripts also holds second_pass.py (an independent rerun of the "next page" test with different measuring choices) and paragraph_ends.py (a recount of pages that end at a paragraph end plus a line-length check).
- The folder page-splitting-scripts holds one Python file that deals a public-domain biology book (Project Gutenberg book number 2009) into four piles and computes the confusion scores.
- The folder word-evolution-scripts holds the copy-and-paste evolution programs and the builder for the 10000 commands. The live slider page now sits in the research folder under the name live-program.html so that visitors can find the live slider page.
- The file many_commands_story_order.js in word-evolution-scripts runs with the command "node many_commands_story_order.js A 7" and the setting BONUS=1 in front of the command turns on the bonus for closeness in the story and the setting UNIQ=0.4 turns on the bonus for words that are not repeats.
- The folder mobius-strip-calculations holds the Python files behind the document named The Mobius Strip Picture of Physics.
- The file bible_quotes_builder.py in word-evolution-scripts cuts chosen passages of the World English Bible into short quotes in Bible order for the Bible version of the live program.
- The files word_level_engine.js and word_level_three_bible_selections.js in word-evolution-scripts run the word-level experiment on three Bible selections and the files whole_books_builder.py and words_of_jesus_builder.py cut the Bible text into short quotes.
- The files key_phrase_builder.py and key_phrase_engine.js and key_phrase_three_bible_selections.js in word-evolution-scripts chop the Bible text into key phrases and run the key phrase experiment.
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts holds three Python files for the number list OEIS A394252: fast_terms.py computes terms exactly (run "python3 fast_terms.py 38") and slow_check.py is a brute-force check for small terms and show_the_30_terms.py prints the 30 earlier terms that add up to the wrongly published term 31.
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds urljoin_test.py (shows which inputs make the Python function urljoin leave the base website) and sentinel_rejected.py and sentinel_enum.py (a marker that type checkers reject and a marker that type checkers accept) and rdflib_prefix_test.py (shows that rdflib keeps one nickname per web address).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds ylim_test.py (rebuilds a matplotlib animation problem and shows the fix) and riemann_step.py (checks a step in Riemann's 1859 paper with numbers) and lawson_check.py (checks whether a formula describes a torus or a Klein bottle).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds smooth_pairs.py (finds every pair of numbers 1 or 2 apart built from a chosen set of primes) and grand_sum.py (checks the sum of all entries of a repeated tensor product) and polar_image.py (rebuilds a polar chart problem and tests a fix).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds scatter_anim.py (rebuilds a scatter animation problem and tests the fix) and celsius_axis.py (tests a second axis in degrees Celsius) and match_stack.py (shows how the Python compiler handles the value being matched).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds dill_parallel.py (parallel work with objects that pickle refuses) and toggle_views.py (switching between two live chart views in one figure) and graph_distance.py (four distance numbers between two graphs drawn in the plane) and layout_test.py (a plot beside an image at the same height) and incircle_check.py (checks a circle squeezed between two lines and an arc).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds zstd_frames.py (packs files into one archive as separate frames) and inference_direction.py (four test lines for two type checkers) and fit_uncertainty.py (fit uncertainties from analytical derivatives) and s3_matrices.py (matrices for the six rearrangements of three things) and a folder named spawn-warning-test (silencing an import warning in a child process).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds lambert_solve.py (solves an equation with a logarithm) and legendre_q.py (checks special functions against known formulas) and svg_by_colour.py (points along SVG paths of one colour) and cornacchia_cost.py (timings for a sum of two squares algorithm) and srgb_webp.py (what Pillow keeps when saving WebP).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds galois_c5.py (Galois group of the eigenvalue field of the 5-cycle graph) and quadruple_link.py (links two formulas for special Pythagorean quadruples) and crypt_heatmap.py (colours cells along a U-shaped outline) and live_plot_speed.py (thinning live data without losing spikes) and echelon_columns.py (identity matrix on chosen columns).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds lll_gram.py (what two PARI lattice functions return) and mock_self.py (stand-in methods that use self) and stop_task.py (stopping a running pool job) and protocol_vs_abc.py (what Protocol and ABC classes enforce) and a folder named forward-test (passing input and output to a child process without hanging).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds lambda_hints.py (a lambda in an annotation that loses a global name) and chess_move.py (line order inside a try block) and centred_bars.py (histogram bars centered on whole numbers) and curve_mod_p2.py (multiplying a point on an elliptic curve modulo p squared) and graph_orbits.py (counting graphs up to relabeling).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds pool_finalize.py (clean-up callbacks in pool workers) and restricted_matching.py (restricted matching number of a graph) and arc_test.py (holes in a thick pygame arc) and condition_test.py (four tests of asyncio.Condition) and completion_times.py (true finish times of thread jobs).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds sigint_nested.py (stopping nested pools on Ctrl-C) and ldap_crl.py (an LDAP address in a certificate) and profile_slots.py (which C calls a profiler sees) and point_in_polygon.py (bulk point-inside-polygon lookups) and a folder named prompt-test (an input prompt hidden from a line reader).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds dulwich_checkout.py (switching a clone to an older revision) and shared_writer.py (one connection per device with an outbox queue) and folium_sectors.py (a sector map with missing fields) and strptime_zone.py (zone names in date parsing) and record_format.py (formatted text for recorded warnings).
- The folder unanswered-questions-scripts also holds audit_fs.py (which file calls raise audit events) and thread_memory.py (memory held by finished futures) and dollar_columns.py (number formats on text cells) and red_missing.py (red text in one spreadsheet column) and crl_parse.py (reading certificate revocation lists).
- The file closeness_rerun.js in word-evolution-scripts reruns the closeness reader with ten new starting points (run "node closeness_rerun.js").
- The files bonus_runs.sh and bonus_runs_read.py in word-evolution-scripts start twenty runs of the many-commands reader under four settings and read the results.
- The file generic_phrase_filter.py in social-media-vibes-scripts counts how many rows of a public comment archive match the list of 10000 generic phrases (put the phrase list beside the file and the archive tables in data/rd and run "python3 generic_phrase_filter.py").
- The folder kindness-games-scripts holds six Python files behind the report on "love your enemies" as a game and each file runs with the command "python3" followed by the file name and prints the average points per turn for each type of player.
- The folder zeta-sums-scripts holds three Python files behind the zeta sums pages (search.py hunts for whole-number formulas and short-formulas.py prints the formulas with the fewest extra pieces and term-by-term-check.py adds up every sum directly) and each file needs the free Python packages mpmath and python-flint (run "python3 search.py 2400").
- The folder zeta-sums-scripts also holds bases.py (searches one base for formulas and runs as "python3 bases.py 3000 plastic 45") and plastic-zeta7.py (formulas for zeta(7) with one extra piece) and check-bases.py (adds up every sum term by term and reads the search output files).
- The folder zeta-sums-scripts also holds ladder-search.py (finds products of the numbers 1 - x^k that equal a power of x and then searches for zeta formulas and runs as "python3 ladder-search.py salem6 12000 400 13") and salem-check.py (rechecks the formulas with a separate routine and reads the output files of ladder-search.py).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts holds ten Python files behind the prime richness pages and each file runs with the command "python3" followed by the file name and needs the free Python package gmpy2 (equation_table.py and fib_prime_sum.py also need mpmath).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts holds divide_table.py (squares and cubes divided with decimals dropped) and equation_table.py (counts for 86 equations with the saved output in equation_table_results.json) and right_end_pieces.py and removed_first_digit.py and reversed_pieces.py and champions.py (the digit pieces of primes) and bumped_pieces.py and bump_conversion.py (the first-digit bump tests) and primorial_lists.py (lists built from the product of the first primes) and fib_prime_sum.py (the sum of 1 over each Fibonacci prime).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts also holds the files behind the two master lists: quad_search.py (hunts for the equation n^2 + n + A with the most primes) and primorial_line.py (multipliers built from many primes) and wild_batch.py (trig functions and rounding rules and modulus and sums and calculus and the digits of constants) and equation_table_bump.py (the 86 equations with the last digit bumped) and master_list_builder.py (joins every result into master_list_data.json).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts also holds running_sums.py and more_sums.py (running totals tested plain and bumped and with the bump carried into the next addition) and sum_search.py (hunts for the best starting number for a sum of multiples and runs as "python3 sum_search.py 15") and zeta_digits.py (digits taken from the zeta sum) and master_list_indicators.py (adds the tested counts and the wobble and the luck odds to every line of the master list).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts also holds meaty.py (equations known for other records and well-known number lists) and weird.py (about 55 invented recipes with digit tricks and bit tricks and geometry) and big_digits.py (the top equations at 100 and 300 and 1000 digits) and low_high.py (37 equations at small outputs and at 100-digit outputs with the correlation).
- The folder prime-richness-scripts also holds log_questions_check.py which rechecks every figure on the page log-questions.md (logarithms and the number e and the constants near 0.56) and needs mpmath.
- The folder prime-richness-scripts also holds no_numbers.py (recipes with several variables and no numbers plus a complete search of one-variable equations whose every coefficient is 1) and dead_bump.py (recipes that give no primes retested with the last digit bumped).
